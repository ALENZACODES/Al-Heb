Mnemonic flashcards

27 cut-out cards, one per letter (22 letters and 5 final forms). Front: the letter in print and in Israeli handwriting. Back: a picture to hang the letter on, its shape, its sound, a short story, the look-alike to watch out for, and a pointed anchor word.

  1. Print on A4 paper at 100% / actual size (turn off "fit to page"). Each card is 2.5 x 3.5 in.
  2. Print double-sided, flip on the LONG edge. Pages alternate front, back; the backs are mirrored so each back lands behind its front.
  3. Cut along the dashed lines (3 x 3 cards per sheet). Use card stock if you can. If your printer is out of register by a few millimetres, trim the cards slightly smaller.
  4. Shuffle, look at the front, say the name and the sound, flip to check, and sort the cards you missed into a second pile.
DRAFT: hooks and anchor words not checked by a Hebrew teacher

Pictures: every illustration is still a labelled placeholder (the image run has not happened). The faint outlined letter is real vector type. The memory hooks are teaching aids, not etymology: the letter-name meanings are real Hebrew words, but the claim that each letter was originally a drawing of that object is only a popular story.

Text source: data/letters/mnemonics.json at build time. Fonts: Noto Serif Hebrew and Gveret Levin (SIL OFL 1.1).

Gimel

03 / 27

Shape An arched back on the right that runs down to a front leg, with a foot kicked out at the lower left.

Sound g as in 'go'

Story Gimel is a Grumpy camel on the Go: arched hump, and one foot kicked out ahead as he stomps toward the water.

Look-alike Nun is a bent line with a flat base and no kicked-out foot. Gimel has the foot sticking out at the bottom left.

גָּמָלgamal
camel

Bet

02 / 27

Shape A house seen from the side: flat roof, back wall on the right, floor that sticks out past the wall, front door open on the left.

Sound b as in 'boy' with a dot inside (bet); v without the dot (vet)

Story Bet is a Bungalow with a Ball inside. The dot is the ball, so the house goes B. Take the ball out and it is Vacant: Vet, with a V.

Look-alike Kaf has soft round corners and no sticking-out floor. Bet has sharp corners and its floor pokes out at the back (right).

בַּיִתbayit
house
draft hook, not checked by a Hebrew teacher

Aleph

01 / 27

Shape A diagonal stroke with an arm reaching up on the right and a foot at the lower left, like an acrobat mid-handstand.

Sound silent letter that carries a vowel (like the tiny pause in 'uh-oh')

Story Aleph is a mime doing a handstand: every move, zero noise. The vowel under it is the audience, doing all the clapping you can hear.

Look-alike Ayin is alef's twin in Israeli and Ashkenazi reading, but ayin is a curved cup with a diagonal lash and no crossing diagonal.

אַבָּאaba
dad
draft hook, not checked by a Hebrew teacher

Vav

06 / 27

Shape One plain straight stroke, as tall as the letters beside it, like a nail or a pole.

Sound v as in 'van'; also the vowels o and u when it carries them

Story Vav is a Vine hanging straight down from the line. Hum 'vvvv' like a vacuum cleaner. It also hooks the next word onto the last: that is why it means 'and'.

Look-alike Zayin has a bar on top. Yod is tiny and floats at the top. Final nun is much longer and drops below the line. Vav is a plain single stroke.

וֶרֶדvered
rose
draft hook, not checked by a Hebrew teacher

He (Hey)

05 / 27

Shape A roof and a right wall, with a short left wall that stops before the roof, leaving a gap.

Sound h as in 'hello' (a breath)

Story He is a Hut with a Hole in the wall. Lean out of the gap and Holler 'Hey!': the h is the breath that escapes through the hole.

Look-alike Chet is fully closed with no gap. Tav has its leg kicked out at the bottom left. He has a gap between the left leg and the roof.

הַרhar

draft hook, not checked by a Hebrew teacher

Dalet

Story Dalet is a Diving board on a Dock post: a flat plank that sticks out over the water, one post under it. Walk the plank and Dive: D!

Look-alike Resh has a rounded shoulder; dalet has a sharp corner and its board sticks out past the post. Final kaf looks like a resh with a long tail.

Chet

08 / 27

Shape A closed doorway: a roof joining a right leg and a left leg, with no gap.

Sound a rough throat 'h', like the ch in 'Chanukah' or Scottish 'loch'

Story Chet is a Closed gate at the edge of a loch, a jug of milk standing beside it: Chhhh! Say it the way you would say 'Chanukah' or 'challah', scraping from the throat.

Look-alike He has a gap in the left wall. Tav has a foot kicked out at the bottom left. Chet is closed all round.

חָלָבchalav

Lamed

12 / 27

Shape The only letter that rises above the line: a hook on top, then a bar and a diagonal leg.

Sound l as in 'lamp'

Story Lamed is a Lamp post with a bent arm that reaches high over the rest of the letters, Lighting the way so everyone can Learn.

Look-alike Nothing else rises above the line, so lamed is rarely confused with another letter.

לֶחֶםlechem

Kaf

11 / 27

Shape A backwards C: a rounded shape with the opening on the left, like a cupped hand or a cup lying on its side.

Sound k with a dot inside (kaf); kh like the letter chet, without the dot (khaf)

Story Kaf is a Cup lying on its side, open on the left (the name kaf means palm of the hand). With a dot a Kernel is in the cup: K. With no dot it is empty and you breathe across it: Khhh.

Look-alike Bet has sharp corners and a floor that sticks out; kaf is soft and round. Final kaf has a long tail. Pe has a curl inside.

כֶּלֶבkelev
dog
draft hook, not checked by a Hebrew teacher

Yod

10 / 27

Shape The smallest letter: a tiny comma that floats at the top of the line.

Sound y as in 'yes'

Story Yod is a Yo-yo bead hanging at the top of the line, the tiniest letter with the loudest 'Yo!'. Yod for Yad (hand): a hand flicking a yo-yo.

Look-alike Vav is a tall straight stroke from the line to the top. Yod is short and floats near the top only.

יָדyad

Sound ts as in 'pizza' (a few traditional Sephardi communities say s)

Story Tsadi is a slice of Pizza with a Bird perched beside it on the box. Pizza has the ts in the middle, and so does Tsadi. Cats has the same ts at the end.

Shape A rounded shape like kaf, with a curl tucked inside at the top left like a tongue behind the lips.

Sound p as in 'pop' with a dot inside (pe); f without the dot (fe)

Story Pe is a mouth pursed to Pop a bubble: with the dot it goes Pop, P! Without the dot the lips go loose and the air Fluffs out: Ffff, F.

Ayin

Shin

21 / 27

Shape Three prongs rising from a curved base, like three candle flames or a trident.

Sound sh as in 'shoe' with the dot on the right (shin); s as in 'sun' with the dot on the left (sin)

Story Shin is three flames on a birthday cake with a finger to the lips: Shhhh! The dot at the top right keeps it shh. A dot on the left makes it Sin: a Sun on the left, Sss.

Look-alike Nothing common: only shin has three prongs. Tsadi is the closest in cursive.

שָׁלוֹםshalom
peace, hello
draft hook, not checked by a Hebrew teacher

Resh

20 / 27

Shape A flat top and a smoothly curved right shoulder with no corner, like a head seen from the side.

Sound r (from the back of the throat in Israeli speech)

Story Resh is a Round head (rosh) from the side: a smooth shoulder with no corner. Rrrr, a growl from the back of the throat.

Look-alike Dalet has a sharp corner at top right and its bar sticks out. Resh is rounded. Final kaf is a resh with a very long tail.

רֹאשׁrosh

Story Qof is a Kite with its long string dangling below the line. K for Kite, K for Qof.

Final Pe

Shape The pe shape with its curl inside and a long right stem that drops below the line.

Sound f, only at the end of a word

Story Final pe is a big Feather held off the tip of a nose, its long quill hanging below the line. Blow Fffff at the end of the word, the way you blow a feather off a spoon (kaf).

Look-alike Final tsadi forks like a Y on top; final pe has the curl and a round top. Pe stays on the line.

Shape One straight stroke that drops below the line, like a nail or a pole.

Sound n, only at the end of a word

Story Final nun is a Nail driven in at the end of the word: one straight stroke below the line. Gan, a garden, ends in a straight pole.

Look-alike Vav is shorter and stays on the line. Final kaf has a bar and a shoulder. Final nun is a plain long stick.
