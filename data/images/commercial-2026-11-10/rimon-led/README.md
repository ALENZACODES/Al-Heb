# Rimon-led store art, 2026-11-10

Rimon is the mascot, so Rimon is big in the frame. The gold Aleph is the logo and stays visible on every piece. It is the same alef app icon (Frank Ruhl Libre, SIL OFL), composited in, never generated. The Aleph set remains the app icon. These pieces sit alongside the tree-led `KEYART_v2_*` files and replace nothing.

Each piece comes as a `.jpg` (q90, for store upload) and a `.webp` (q85, for the web). Metadata is stripped; exiftool finds no EXIF, XMP or IPTC. Rimon follows the frozen design: round red pomegranate, gold calyx crown, olive leaf ears, navy scarf, stick limbs, gender-neutral. The screenshot hero frames leave the top band free for a caption.
