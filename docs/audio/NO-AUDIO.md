# Audio-less experience (0 recorded clips)

Three honest levels, decided by `audioAccess()` in `src/shell/audio-access.js` (re-exported from `src/shell/ui.js`):

| level | when | shown |
|---|---|---|
| file | manifest entry is recorded/tts or synthetic (incl. outside-source clips, labelled "outside generator ... unreviewed") | "Recorded by a human reader" or "Synthetic voice (Israeli only), not a recorded teacher" (src/shell/audio-labels.js) |
| device | no clip, a he-IL browser voice exists or may exist (voices not yet loaded) | "Device voice, not a recorded teacher; Israeli-style only" (never Sephardi/Ashkenazi) |
| none | no clip and no usable voice (no speechSynthesis, only non-Hebrew voices, unknown item) | "No audio yet" plus the written respelling where the view has one |

- `audioButton(h, ctx, {kind,id,label,respelling,text,name})` is the shared button: a real button only for file/device, otherwise a one-line "No audio yet". `text` is the pointed Hebrew the screen shows (the device voice may read it when the item is not in the recording list); `name` is added to the accessible name (e.g. the English gloss) so a list of Listen buttons does not repeat one name.
- One sound bar at the top (`.sound-bar` in `index.html`, wired by `wireSoundBar` in `src/shell/audio-access.js`, called from `src/app.js`): the status sentence, a "Details" link to `#/how` and a dismiss button. Dismissal is kept in localStorage `alef.audioBannerSeen.v1`; the bar returns for a non-idle status (e.g. the recording list could not be loaded).
- Tradition mismatch: the browser voice is Israeli only; `browserVoiceAnyway` still decides whether it speaks for Sephardi/Ashkenazi settings.
- Views on the shared button (`audioButton`, or a thin wrapper over it): `curriculum/audio-ui.js` (playButton, wording), `curriculum/ex-ui.js` (`voiceButton`: gender screens), `curriculum/ladder.view.js` (Listen again), `curriculum/intermediate.view.js`, `placement/placement.view.js`, `vocab/vocab.view.js`, `vocab/themes.view.js`, `israel/israel.view.js`, `sprint/sprint.view.js` (hear-the-meaning question), `service/service.view.js`, `morning/morning.view.js` (Modeh Ani), `wordtrace/wordtrace.view.js`, `pronunciation/ui.js` (vowel board, niqqud dial, dot tile), `pronunciation/soundgap.view.js`, `letters/atlas.view.js` (the Hear buttons), `corpus/read.view.js` (Play line, phrasebook Play) and `torahread` (`hearButton` in `torahread/ui.js`, used by the ladder, aliyah and words views). They all follow the same rules: the device voice is Israeli only (a Sephardi or Ashkenazi setting shows "No audio yet" plus the respelling when "Use my browser's voice anyway" is off; it is on by default, and then the button is labelled "Device voice, not a recorded teacher; Israeli-style only"), and the liturgical-name check runs before anything is spoken. With no clip and no usable voice there is no button, only one "No audio yet" line. With "I do not use audio" on there is neither.
- Not on the shared button, on purpose:
  - Tapping a word or a tile is itself the play control, and it reports what happened after the tap: the words and Play all in `corpus/read.view.js`, the letter tiles in `letters/atlas.view.js`, `pronunciation/ui.js` `tryPlay` (vowel signs, dot tile, syllable chips: plays only when a clip exists), and the automatic play when a word-trace result opens.
  - `listen/listen.view.js`: a listening exercise is only offered when clips exist, because a device voice reading Modern Israeli Hebrew would test the wrong thing; its Play and replay buttons stay.
  - `speak/speak.js`: it needs slow-rate speech and its own timeout, so it keeps its own Listen control and says after the tap what was played ("the browser's voice, not a recording").
  - `firstday/` (Hear it): says after the tap which kind of sound was heard; not moved yet.
  - `curriculum/ex-ui.js` still exports `audioStatus`, which the gender and ladder screens use to decide whether to offer a listening question.
- Caller text: `plan()` accepts `o.text` as a last resort, after every file step and after the manifest's own text. So an id that is not in the recording list can still use the device voice with the text the screen shows, under the same Israeli-only and name checks (tests: `tests/audio.access.test.js`, "caller text").

Optional `audioButton` options added for these views: `noneClass` (class of the "No audio yet" line), `buttonId` and `attrs` (hooks a view or test addresses).

Tests: `tests/audio.access.test.js` (fake audio/speech objects), `tests/audio.views.test.js` (morning, corpus read, torahread words), and the file/device/none cases added to the view tests of israel, sprint, service, wordtrace, soundgap, pronunciation round 4, atlas and the curriculum ladder. They use fake DOM, audio and speech objects; they show which control is offered and what is passed to the player, not real sound. Browser pass: speechSynthesis stubbed off/on, banner text verified for both; route-level button counts were not conclusive (0 on the routes tried).
