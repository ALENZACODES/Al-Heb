## Tradition checklists (score authenticity against these)

### Israeli (modern)

- Sephardi-based **standard Israeli** pronunciation.
- **tav** always **“t”** (with or without dagesh — not “s”).
- **kamatz** = **“a”**.
- **ayin** lightly sounded or unsounded (modern norm OK).
- Stress usually **final syllable**.
- **resh** as in modern Hebrew.
- Vowels and pacing fit everyday Israeli Hebrew, not liturgical Ashkenazi colouring.

### Sephardi (liturgical)

- **ayin** genuinely sounded (not collapsed to silence).
- **het** distinct from **khaf**.
- **kamatz** = **“a”**.
- **tav** = **“t”**.
- **Final-syllable stress** predominant.
- Vowels **pure** (no diphthong drift toward Ashkenazi “oy/ay” habits).
- Liturgical dignity without Ashkenazi tav→s or kamatz→o.

### Ashkenazi (liturgical)

- **tav without dagesh** = **“s”**.
- **kamatz** = **“o”** (as in “Shabbos”).
- **tsere** = **“ay”**.
- **holam** = **“oy” / “ei”** per the community norm stated for this audition (note which).
- Stress often **penultimate** (e.g. “TOY-ras”, “SHAB-bos”).
- **ayin** often silent or lightly ng-coloured per that same community norm.
- Must **not** sound like modern Israeli with a thin Ashkenazi glaze.

**Hard promise:** Israeli-pronounced audio under a Sephardi or Ashkenazi label is a defect **worse than silence**. Prefer UNFILLED + recording kit over a fake tradition label.

## Decision rule (frozen)

A candidate **wins** a tradition×voice slot only if **all** of the following hold after three independent judges:

1. **Three-judge average overall ≥ 8**
2. **Authenticity ≥ 8 from at least 2 of 3 judges**
3. **No single judge scores authenticity below 6**

### Ties / failures

1. Judges may confer **only on disputed criteria** (not wholesale re-ranking by preference).
2. Re-audition the **top two** on a **fresh 12-item** set (same blind protocol).
3. If still unresolved → slot **UNFILLED** with written reasons + human recording kit (`recording-kit-<slot>.md`).
