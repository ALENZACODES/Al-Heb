# Torah coverage report (2026-11-07)

Report only: nothing was imported, rendered or changed by this document. It answers two questions: what Hebrew and English Torah text ALEF holds today, and what the owner's "whole Torah in Hebrew and English with slow replay" still needs. Counts are read from the files named; where a count comes from a generated doc rather than from a file in this tree, the doc is named.

- Per-parashah: `index.json` holds all 54 portions with verse ranges and aliyot (Hebcal leyning 5.2.0), tiled with no gaps or overlaps over the whole Torah. Text exists for the 12 Genesis portions (Bereshit to Vayechi) and for none of the other 42 (Shemot to Vezot Haberakhah).

- Each verse carries `durMs: {m, f}` and `durMsEn: {m, f}` slots for audio timing. All are `null` in every Genesis chapter file: no Torah clip exists.
- The reader already has the chapter screens (book list, chapter list, verse list, Play chapter in Hebrew and in English, Play verse), wired to audio ids `torah-<book>-<chapter>-<verse>` (kind `line`), `torah-<book>-<chapter>` (kind `torah-chapter`) and `torah-en-<book>-<chapter>` (kind `torah-chapter-en`), read from `data/audio/manifest-torah.json`. That manifest is an empty list today, so every Play button reports "no audio" honestly.

## 2. What "the whole Torah in Hebrew and English with slow replay" needs

### 2a. Text: 137 chapter files (4 books)
- `node tools/torah/fetch_torah.mjs` already knows all five books (it counted and validated their grids) and built only Genesis in its 2026-11-07 run. Running it for Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers and Deuteronomy is the whole text job: no new code, network access to tanach.us (UXLC XML) and the Sefaria v3 API (pinned JPS 1917) is required, and the tool asserts the licence string on every chapter. Expected output: 137 files, about 3.2 MB, plus `index.json` with `chaptersAvailable` updated and `docs/torah/SOURCES.md` regenerated.
- Verification the tool already performs: skeleton equality per verse against the Sefaria guide segments, word-stream consumption per book, portion tiling. It should be re-run and its report committed.
- Nothing to proofread by hand in the text itself (it is a copy of UXLC and JPS 1917), but the JPS 1917 English is a 1917 translation ("thee/thou"); the reader already says so (`TORAH_TEXT_LABEL`).

### 2b. Audio: 5,846 Hebrew verses + 187 Hebrew chapters + 187 English chapters
- The reader's playback design is per chapter with per-verse highlighting from `durMs` (chapter clip plus verse durations) and per-verse Hebrew clips for "Play verse". So the minimum for the whole Torah is 187 Hebrew chapter clips and 187 English chapter clips per voice, plus the per-verse duration tables; per-verse Hebrew clips (5,846 per voice) are needed for "Play verse" and for the slow replay of a single verse.
- Volume (from `docs/torah/SOURCES.md`, counted from the fetched grids): Hebrew 644,747 spoken-form characters per voice; English 811,902 characters per voice. With two voices (Ari m, Noa f) that is about 1.29 M Hebrew and 1.62 M English characters of synthesis, before any re-renders. The Israeli reading is the only tradition the reader offers for Torah (`TORAH_AUDIO_LABEL`); Sephardi and Ashkenazi would double or triple the Hebrew count and are not planned there.
- Storage: at the app's current clip bitrates (see `docs/audio/RANGE-AND-OFFLINE.md`) a chapter clip runs to several minutes; the whole Torah per voice is in the order of 50-60 hours of Hebrew plus 60-70 hours of English at reading pace, which cannot be precached and must stay range-served on demand, as the Torah manifest already is (`ON_DEMAND_FILES` in `tools/build-precache.mjs`).
- Manifest: `data/audio/manifest-torah.json` entries for the ids above, with `durMs` written back into each chapter file so the verse highlight can follow the clip (`chapterPosition` in `src/torahreader/model.js`).
- Honesty line: every Torah clip would be synthetic (status `tts`, "a reading, not a chant"); the reader's labels already say this and must stay.

### 2c. Slow replay: no new clips needed
- The shared player (`src/audio/player.js`, 2026-11-07) plays any file at 1× or at the one slow speed 0.7× with `preservesPitch`, chosen per call (`options.rate`) or in Settings (`clipRate`, default normal). Torah verses and chapters go through `ctx.audio.play`, so slow replay works the moment clips exist, with no second rendering. What the reader screen lacks is a visible per-verse "Slower" control like the one added to the story reader this round (`src/books/reader.speed.js`); that is a small view change, not a data job. The verse highlight timing (`chapterPosition`) must scale by the rate when slow is on, which it does not do yet (it reads `durMs` at 1×).

### 2d. Order of work suggested
1. Fetch the four remaining books (one tool run, network); commit the 137 chapter files, `index.json`, `SOURCES.md` and the fetch report.
2. Add the per-verse "Slower" control and rate-aware highlighting to the Torah reader (view only).
3. Render Hebrew chapter and verse clips for Genesis first (the text is in the tree), write `manifest-torah.json` and `durMs`; then the other books as their text lands; English chapters after Hebrew.
4. Keep the per-parashah view (already in the reader) as the way in: Calendar and the day pages link to `/torahread` today and could link straight to the portion's first chapter once all 54 portions have text.
