## pu-greetings-p01: Hello and Goodbye

**Grammar: The adjective comes after the noun and matches it.** In Hebrew the describing word follows the noun: בֹּקֶר טוֹב is morning good. It also matches the noun: צָהֳרַיִם is a plural form, so it takes טוֹבִים, not טוֹב.

## pu-greetings-p06: Shabbat and Holiday Greetings

*Learn page.* Use the right greeting on Shabbat and holidays.

**Grammar: Masculine and feminine nouns.** Every noun is masculine or feminine, and its adjective follows. שָׁבוּעַ (week) is masculine: שָׁבוּעַ טוֹב. שָׁנָה (year) is feminine: שָׁנָה טוֹבָה. Many feminine nouns end in ה.

**Culture.** Shabbat shalom is said from Friday afternoon through Shabbat. Shavua tov is said once Shabbat ends on Saturday night. Shana tova belongs to Rosh Hashana.

| Hebrew | Transliteration | English | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| שַׁבָּת שָׁלוֹם | shabbat shalom | peaceful Sabbath (greeting from Friday afternoon to Saturday) | `greetings-07` tts, f+m |
| שָׁבוּעַ טוֹב | shavua tov | good week | `greetings-08` tts, f+m |
| חַג שָׂמֵחַ | chag sameach | happy holiday | `greetings-09` tts, f+m |
| שָׁנָה טוֹבָה | shana tova | happy new year (Rosh Hashana greeting) | `greetings-10` tts, f+m |

**Speak.**

- It is Friday afternoon. Greet the people at the bakery. → שַׁבָּת שָׁלוֹם (shabbat shalom) · audio `greetings-07`
- Shabbat has just ended on Saturday night. → שָׁבוּעַ טוֹב (shavua tov) · audio `greetings-08`

## pu-fillers-p05: Slower, Please

**Grammar: אֶפְשָׁר + to-verb: a polite request with no gender.** אֶפְשָׁר means it is possible. Add a verb that starts with לְ or לִ (to speak, to write) and you have a polite request that is the same for everyone: אֶפְשָׁר לְדַבֵּר לְאַט?
