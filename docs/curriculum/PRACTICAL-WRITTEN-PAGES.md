## pu-greetings-p06: Shabbat and Holiday Greetings

*Learn page.* Use the right greeting on Shabbat and holidays.

**Grammar: Masculine and feminine nouns.** Every noun is masculine or feminine, and its adjective follows. שָׁבוּעַ (week) is masculine: שָׁבוּעַ טוֹב. שָׁנָה (year) is feminine: שָׁנָה טוֹבָה. Many feminine nouns end in ה.

**Culture.** Shabbat shalom is said from Friday afternoon through Shabbat. Shavua tov is said once Shabbat ends on Saturday night. Shana tova belongs to Rosh Hashana.

| שָׁבוּעַ טוֹב | shavua tov | good week | `greetings-08` tts, f+m |
