Belts: Krav Maga style (IKMF-style layout). White, Yellow, Orange, Green, Blue, Brown and Black belts, with rank patches P (Practitioner), G (Graduate) and E (Expert); the stripes on a patch give the rank number. ## Reading and prayer track

71 lessons in course order, with Krav Maga style belts and rank patches (P Practitioner, G Graduate, E Expert). Each rank is the next run of lessons; finish them to earn it. It holds 20 of the 50 I- (Intermediate) lessons; the other 30 are on the Israel track.

### White belt · P0 (4 lessons: r01–r04, 80 min)

Goal of the rank: Reads Shabbat Shalom: Shin, Bet, Tav, Lamed, final Mem and Vav with kamatz, patach, cholam and dagesh.

- **r01** Shin and Bet: your first two letters (20 min)
  - Name and sound Shin (sh) and Bet (b with the dot inside, v without)
  - Read kamatz and patach with them: both are 'ah' (Ashkenazi reads kamatz as 'aw')
- **r02** Tav and the dot in Bet: Shabbat (20 min)
  - Read שַׁבָּת (Shabbat; Ashkenazi says Shabbos)
  - Know the dot inside Bet hardens it to 'b'
- **r03** Lamed, final Mem and Vav: Shalom (25 min)
  - Read שָׁלוֹם
  - Vav with a dot above = 'oh'
  - Recognise the final-mem shape
- **r04** Shabbat Shalom: review (15 min)
  - Read שַׁבָּת שָׁלוֹם smoothly without help
  - Pass the White belt · P0 check: 6 letters, 3 vowels

### Yellow belt · P1 (5 lessons: r05–r09, 105 min)

Goal of the rank: Meets the Priestly Blessing; Yod, Aleph, He, Resh and Mem with chirik and tzere.

- **r05** Meet the Priestly Blessing (listen and spot) (15 min)
  - Hear the whole Priestly Blessing (Numbers 6:24-26)
  - Spot the letters you already know (Shin, Bet, Lamed, Vav and final Mem) in the three lines
  - Understand this is the page-1 text you will fully decode by lesson 27
- **r06** Yod and the 'ee' vowel (20 min)
  - Read chirik as 'ee' (the words here have a yod after it; chirik without a yod sounds the same)
- **r07** Aleph and He (25 min)
  - Know Aleph is silent carrier of its vowel
  - Know final-He after kamatz is silent
- **r08** Tzere: the 'e' / 'ay' vowel (20 min)
  - Read tzere with and without yod: 'e' as in bed (Israeli, Sephardi) or 'ay' as in they (Ashkenazi)
- **r09** Resh and Mem (25 min)
  - Tell Resh by its rounded corner (Dalet, taught in r18, has a sharp corner)
  - Use medial vs final Mem correctly

### Yellow belt · P2 (5 lessons: r10–r14, 120 min)

- **r13** Shuruk and Kubutz: the 'oo' vowels (25 min)
  - Read Vav+dot-inside as 'oo'

- **r14** Shva at the start of a syllable (25 min)
  - Read a shva at the start of a word as a quick 'e'

### Orange belt · P3 (5 lessons: r15–r19, 115 min)

  - Tell Pe with dagesh (p, as in פֶּה) from final Pe (f, as in אַף)

  - Tell Dalet from Resh and Gimel from Nun
- **r19** Ayin (20 min)
  - Ayin: silent in Ashkenazi reading and in most Israeli speech (sometimes a light catch in the throat); many Sephardi and Mizrahi readers sound it deep in the throat

### Black belt · E3 (3 lessons: I-08–I-10, Intermediate reading, 95 min)

  - Read the first paragraph of Aleinu at the speed it is said
  - Find the words where many congregations bow
  - Say the closing 'ein od' line from memory

- **I-09** Adon Olam: the closing hymn line by line (30 min)
  - Sing the first and last verses of Adon Olam from the page without stopping
  - Recognise the three-tense line 'hayah, hoveh, yihyeh'
  - Know Yigdal as the other hymn that often opens or closes a service
- **I-10** Mi Shebeirach, healing and thanksgiving: the blessings said for people (35 min)
  - Recognise the opening of Mi Shebeirach and answer it
  - Answer Birkat Hagomel with the congregation's response
  - Answer Shehecheyanu with Amen, and answer the Priestly Blessing said by the leader with 'Ken yehi ratzon' (when kohanim bless, the answer is Amen)

### Black belt · E4 (5 lessons: I-26–I-30, Intermediate reading, 165 min)

Goal of the rank: Intermediate reading: the full Torah service, Shabbat-morning Amidah responses, Yom Kippur, Pesach and Sukkot.

- **I-26** The full Torah service in order: ark, reading, lifting, returning (45 min)
  - Name the twelve moments of a Shabbat-morning Torah service in order
  - Join the lines the congregation sings: Ein Kamocha, Gadlu, Lecha Adonai, Etz Chaim
  - Follow the reading: aliyah blessings, lifting, haftarah, returning the scroll
- **I-27** Shabbat-morning Amidah responses: Kedushah, Sim Shalom, closing verses (35 min)
  - Answer the Kedushah at each cue
  - Say Amen after the peace blessing
  - Recognise the three closing verses of the Amidah in order
- **I-28** Yom Kippur: the Shema line said aloud and the words of the day (30 min)
  - Know the one day a year when 'Baruch shem' is said aloud and answer it
  - Recognise the Yom Kippur words: fast, atonement, shofar at the close, forgiveness
  - Greet people with 'gmar chatimah tovah' on Yom Kippur (not only 'shanah tovah', which belongs to Rosh Hashana and the days around it)
  - Know that Kol Nidrei and most of the Yom Kippur liturgy are not in Aleph yet
- **I-29** Pesach: the seder blessings and the words of the festival (30 min)
  - Say the wine blessing and the Shehecheyanu that open the seder
  - Recognise the Pesach insert and the close of the festival Kiddush, including 'a remembrance of the exodus from Egypt' and 'who makes Israel and the seasons holy'
  - Wish people 'chag kasher vesameach' or 'chag sameach', and know which seder texts Aleph still does not hold
- **I-30** Sukkot: sukkah words and the blessings that carry over (25 min)
  - Greet people with chag sameach and recognise the word sukkah
  - Say Shehecheyanu, the wine blessing, the Sukkot Kiddush insert, and the blessing to dwell in the sukkah
  - Know that the lulav blessing, Hoshanot, and the rest of Hallel are not in Aleph yet

### Black belt · E5 (5 lessons: I-31–I-35, Intermediate reading, 145 min)

Goal of the rank: Intermediate reading: Purim, Hanukkah, Birkat Hamazon openings, the journey blessings and Shabbat-day Kiddush.

- **I-31** Purim: the miracle blessing and the words of the day (25 min)
  - Say the blessing for miracles, whose wording is shared by Purim and Hanukkah (Aleph stores that wording with the Hanukkah source)
  - Greet and toast in Hebrew
  - Know that the Megillah reading is not in Aleph yet
- **I-32** Hanukkah: the three blessings, Hanerot Halalu and Maoz Tzur (35 min)
  - Say the candle-lighting blessing and answer Amen
  - Follow the order of blessings on the first night
  - Read the first lines of Hanerot Halalu and Maoz Tzur
  - Know that the candle blessing here follows Edot HaMizrach, 'ner Chanukah'; Ashkenaz often says 'ner shel Chanukah'
- **I-33** Birkat Hamazon openings: Zimun, the first blessing and Shir Hamaalot (30 min)
  - Lead or answer the Zimun invitation
  - Read the first blessing of Birkat Hamazon
  - Recognise Shir Hamaalot, sung before the blessing on Shabbat
- **I-34** Before and after a journey: Hagomel, peace and the vocabulary of the road (25 min)
  - Say Hagomel after a safe journey and answer the congregation's reply
  - Wish a traveller peace in Hebrew
  - Know that Tefillat HaDerekh (the traveller's prayer) is not in Aleph yet
- **I-35** Shabbat-day Kiddush: V'shamru, Zachor, wine and bread (30 min)
  - Read V'shamru and Zachor as the Kiddush before the Shabbat lunch
  - Say the wine and bread blessings and answer Amen
  - Hear how the Shabbat-day Kiddush differs from Friday night

## Israel and everyday Hebrew track

54 lessons in course order, in stages. This track has no belts. It holds 30 of the 50 I- (Intermediate) lessons; the other 20 are on the Reading track.

### Stage 1 (9 lessons, 210 min)

- **il01** Hello, thanks, sorry (20 min)
  - Greet and thank in Hebrew without switching to English
- **il02** Fillers that make you sound local (20 min)
  - Use 5 fillers at the right moments
  - Hear stress and rhythm rather than read letters
- **il03** Introducing yourself (25 min)
  - Say your name, where you are from and ask the same
- **il04** Numbers 1-10 (25 min)
  - Count 1-10 and understand spoken prices
- **il05** Numbers 11-100 and shekel prices (25 min)
  - Say and understand prices up to 100 shekels
- **il06** Cafe: ordering a coffee (25 min)
  - Order a drink politely and ask for the bill
- **il07** Cafe and menu words (25 min)
  - Read a short menu by ear and by sight
- **il08** Shop: asking and paying (25 min)
  - Ask the price, ask if something exists, say it is too expensive
- **il09** Paying, change and bags (20 min)
  - Handle the checkout exchange

### Stage 2 (9 lessons, 245 min)

- **il10** Taxi and public transport (25 min)
  - Say where you are going and ask the price or the stop
- **il11** Directions (30 min)
  - Ask for and understand directions
  - Give a taxi driver a street and a number
- **il12** Days, time and dates (30 min)
  - Tell the time and arrange to meet
- **il13** Small talk: how are you (25 min)
  - Open and keep a 3-turn chat
- **il14** Family and who you are (25 min)
  - Describe your family in 5 sentences
- **il15** Weather and seasons (20 min)
  - Chat about the weather
- **il16** Present tense: the verb that does the work (35 min)
  - Conjugate five pa'al/pi'el verbs in the present (m./f./pl.)
- **il17** Hotel and booking (25 min)
  - Check in and ask about breakfast time
- **il18** Help, health and not understanding (30 min)
  - Repair a conversation instead of switching language

### Stage 3 (36 lessons, includes 30 I- lessons, 995 min)

- **il19** Past tense (A2/B1 bridge) (35 min)
  - Tell what you did yesterday in 5 sentences
- **il20** Future tense and plans (35 min)
  - Make and confirm a plan for next week
- **il21** Opinions and preferences (30 min)
  - Give a reason and disagree politely
- **il22** Phone, WhatsApp and appointments (30 min)
  - Set up and move an appointment by phone or WhatsApp
- **il23** Sound less like an outsider: pronunciation markers (30 min)
  - Practise the three sounds that mark a foreign accent: resh, chet and word stress
- **il24** Capstone: an A2-B1 conversation (40 min)
  - Hold a 5-minute conversation covering greetings, a purchase, directions and a plan
- **I-11** Bus and train: tickets, platforms and getting off (30 min)
  - Ask which bus goes somewhere and whether this is the right one
  - Buy or validate a ticket and ask for the platform
  - Say that you are getting off at the next stop
- **I-12** Shuk bargaining: prices, discounts and 'I'll take it' (30 min)
  - Ask a price and react to it (expensive, cheap)
  - Ask for a discount politely and close the deal
  - Pay in cash and ask for a bag
- **I-13** Cafe ordering: milk, sugar, sizes and the bill (25 min)
  - Order a coffee with the milk, sugar and size you want
  - Answer 'eat here or take away?'
  - Ask for the bill and pay by card
- **I-14** Pharmacy: pain, fever, prescriptions and how often to take it (30 min)
  - Describe a headache, sore throat, stomach ache or fever
  - Ask for something for it and whether a prescription is needed
  - Understand how many times a day to take it
- **I-15** Directions: streets, corners and 'how do I get to' (30 min)
  - Ask how to get somewhere and whether it is far
  - Follow 'straight, left, right, next to, opposite'
  - Say you are lost and ask to be shown on the map
- **I-16** Phone and SIM: numbers, messages, battery and signal (30 min)
  - Ask for and give a phone number
  - Ask about internet, a password and a charger
  - Handle a bad line: 'one moment', 'I can't hear'
- **I-17** Apartment viewing: rooms, floor, rent and the landlord (30 min)
  - Say you are looking for an apartment and ask how many rooms
  - Ask about the floor, balcony, parking and the monthly price
  - Ask about the contract and the landlord
- **I-18** Army slang basics: how soldiers and their friends talk (25 min)
  - Recognise the basic army words you will hear from Israelis in their twenties
  - Use the slang greetings and reactions at the right register
  - Know which of these not to say to officials or elders
- **I-19** Shabbat invitation: being invited, what to bring, saying thanks (25 min)
  - Accept a Shabbat dinner invitation and ask what to bring
  - Ask what time and say Shabbat shalom on arrival
  - Compliment the food and thank the hosts
- **I-20** Hospital and clinic: doctor, appointment, insurance, 'where does it hurt' (30 min)
  - Say you need a doctor and where it hurts
  - Ask for an appointment and show your insurance or passport
  - Call for an ambulance in an emergency
- **I-21** Bank: accounts, cash machines, exchanging money and signing (30 min)
  - Ask where a bank or cash machine is and when it closes
  - Say you want to open an account, withdraw or exchange money
  - Understand 'sign here' and ask about fees and interest
- **I-22** Post office: parcels, letters, stamps and waiting your turn (25 min)
  - Say you want to send a parcel or letter abroad
  - Ask how long it takes and how much it costs
  - Take a number and collect a parcel
- **I-23** Taxi: destination, meter, 'slower' and the receipt (25 min)
  - Tell the driver where to go and ask for the meter
  - Ask the price in advance and ask the driver to slow down or stop
  - Ask for a receipt and thank the driver
- **I-24** Weather: hot, cold, rain, sun and what to take (25 min)
  - Ask about and describe today's weather

  - Talk about seasons and what to take with you

  - Introduce yourself and say where you are from and how long you have been here
  - Ask what someone does and say you are learning Hebrew
  - End the conversation and keep in touch
- **I-36** Work: office, meeting, boss and interview (30 min)

  - Use 'one moment' and 'I don't understand' politely at work
- **I-37** University: courses, lectures, exams and scholarships (30 min)
  - Say what you study and where
  - Ask when a class or exam is and where a room is
  - Ask about a scholarship
- **I-38** Dating and going out: asking someone out, plans and compliments (25 min)
  - Suggest a plan (dinner, a film) politely
  - Accept or turn down an invitation
  - Give and receive a compliment without sounding stiff

  - Ask where parking or fuel is

  - Understand stop, slow and straight on the road
- **I-40** Hiking: trail, mountain, stream, water and the map (25 min)
  - Ask the way to a trail and how long it is
  - Say you need water and a hat
  - Read a map request and the simple words for a hike
- **I-41** Beach: sea, sand, towel and swimming (20 min)
  - Say you are going to the beach
  - Ask about the water and say it is hot or cold
  - Understand 'danger' and ask for help
- **I-42** Kids: asking about children, school and play (25 min)
  - Say how many children you have and ask about theirs
  - Talk about school, a teacher and a game
  - Compliment a child and a parent politely
- **I-43** Supermarket: aisles, prices, bags and the checkout (25 min)
  - Find an item and ask its price
  - Pay at the checkout in cash or by card
  - Ask for a bag and a receipt
- **I-44** Repairs and the landlord: something is broken (25 min)
  - Tell the landlord something is broken
  - Ask when someone can come and say if it is urgent
  - Name the common household things that break

  - Place a simple order by phone
  - Give the address, floor and door
  - Ask how long it takes and thank the courier
- **I-46** Emergencies: police, ambulance, fire and calling for help (30 min)
  - Shout for help and say what happened
  - Ask someone to call the police or an ambulance
  - Say you lost your phone or passport and that it is urgent
- **I-47** News headlines: government, elections, war and peace, prices (25 min)
  - Recognise the commonest headline words
  - Ask what the news is today
  - Say 'there is / there isn't' about prices and peace
- **I-48** Banking forms: name, number, signature and date (25 min)
  - Read the field names on a simple form
  - Fill name, address, phone, date and signature in Hebrew words
  - Ask where to sign
- **I-49** Social media: posting, messages, photos and passwords (20 min)
  - Send and ask for a message or picture
  - Ask for someone's profile and say 'one moment'

- **I-50** Holiday greetings by festival, plus everyday wishes (25 min)
  - Choose the right greeting for Shabbat, a festival, Rosh Hashana, Yom Kippur and after Shabbat
