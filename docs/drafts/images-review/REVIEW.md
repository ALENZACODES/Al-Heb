# Review of draft pictures

Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is approved. No human has reviewed these pictures.

## Scope and method

- 1500 pictures, each re-saved as a neutral-named 512-pixel copy (`img-0001.png` to `img-1500.png`).
- Numbers are positions in the original sort order. No mapping to the original names is kept in this repository.
- Every picture was looked at on a contact sheet (5 x 4 grid, 20 pictures per sheet, 75 sheets) and given a subject, problems and a verdict.
- No exact duplicates were found (pixel hash comparison). Some near-duplicates exist (same scene with small changes).
- Verdicts: usable-draft, needs-redo, reject. Nothing was judged reject.

Other things to check by a person:
- A few scenes contain small religious items (scrolls, prayer shawls, a ram's horn, candles, a crescent with stars). These look intended. A person should confirm they are drawn respectfully.
- One scene shows a small star symbol on a door, in a dense town picture. Tiny details like this cannot be judged on a contact sheet.
- A few pictures show a person's face cropped on purpose (lips and nose only).
- Several hands show olive-coloured patches on the skin that look like stains.

## Style consistency

- One clear house style: flat vector shapes, thick dark outlines, a small palette (navy, gold, brick red, olive green, cream).
- Recurring characters: a bearded man with a chain necklace, a woman with curly dark hair and gold earrings, a boy, older women and men, and a crowned pomegranate mascot.
- Mixed styles that stand out: black silhouettes (28), grey badges (10), flat colour blobs (about 10), and card templates with an empty caption box (30). These break the set.
- Many pictures are wide strips, not squares. The strips sit on white padding in the sheets, so the crop differs from the square ones.
- No readable letters, words or logos were seen. Signs, labels, ribbons, licence plates and certificates are blank. At 512 pixels, very small text could be missed.
- No recognisable real person was seen. Faces are generic cartoon faces.
- No inappropriate content was seen.

## Which pictures belong to which set

- The content is everyday vocabulary and scene pictures: objects, food, places, actions, feelings, family, travel, health, shopping, weather, and holiday and farm scenes. All 1500 share the same look.
- Only 85 short prompts were available to describe what pictures were meant to show. They carry no picture numbers, so they cannot be matched to specific pictures here.
- A few dozen pictures look like letter-shaped mnemonic objects (a table, a nail, a doorway, a hook, a key, a corner). That may mean they belong to a letter set. This is a guess ("cannot tell").
- No picture was judged unrelated to the set by style. Whether each picture belongs to a particular word or letter list cannot be decided from the pictures alone.

## Limits

- Contact sheets only. Each picture was seen at about 380 pixels wide. Hands, feet, small text and tiny background figures were not checked closely.
- One reviewer pass, no second opinion, no human review.
- Subject names are short descriptions and can be wrong when a picture is abstract.
- The real-place verdicts are impressions of resemblance. Nothing was checked against photos.
- Counts per problem are from keywords in `review.csv` and are approximate.
- Pictures are 512-pixel copies. The originals were not changed and are not included.
