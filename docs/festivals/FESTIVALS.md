# Festivals screens: what they are, where the data comes from, what was and was not checked

Files: `src/festivals/model.js` (pure), `src/festivals/festivals.view.js`, `src/festivals/build.mjs` (dev tool that builds the vocabulary packs), `data/festivals/{vocab,links,shabbat-ready}.json`, tests in `tests/festivals.test.js`. Routes: see `ROUTES.md`. Everything is a DRAFT; nobody with Hebrew or halachic standing has reviewed it.

## 1. This week in the Jewish year (card)
- Torah portion of the Shabbat of this week (today, if it is Saturday): `parshaOnShabbat` over `data/calendar/parsha.json`. The tables are Hebcal-derived (see `docs/curriculum/calendar-notes.md`), so the check against the repo's Hebcal snapshot is a consistency check, not an independent source. Diaspora schedule unless the chosen city is in Israel or the setting says Israel. No separate annual-cycle algorithm was written: the cycle is in the data. Cross-check in `tests/festivals.test.js`: every portion of Hebrew years 5787, 5788 and 5789 (diaspora, 144 Shabbatot with a portion) and the whole of 5787 for Israel equals the snapshot, and every other Saturday is reported as having no regular portion.
- Next festival within 30 days (inclusive; one under way counts): calendar.js holidays of kind major, minor or fast that have a word pack. Compared on every day of 2026-2028 (1,096 days, reform mode) with a festival list built from the Hebcal snapshot, not from calendar.js. Other modes (traditional, israel) are covered by `tests/calendar.festivals.test.js` for the dates and by one traditional-mode case here.
- Candle lighting: the reminders path (`buildReminders`), so the card and the reminders agree, including the festival eves and second nights fixed in `docs/qa/CALENDAR-QA.md`. Shown from now to the first Shabbat. Toronto equals the Hebcal snapshot to the minute on all 34 Fridays; Jerusalem uses 40 minutes. No city set gives a prompt, not a guess.
- "Today" is the device's local date; the Calendar screen uses the city's zone instead. They differ only for a learner viewing a far-away city's times.
- Links appear only when `ctx.hasRoute` says the route exists and the id exists in the loaded data (registry text ids, lesson ids). Festival texts linked: Hanukkah blessings, Hanerot Halalu, Maoz Tzur, Shehecheyanu for the pilgrim festivals and Rosh Hashanah, Kiddush wine, Torah blessings for Shavuot; lessons I-29 to I-32 and I-50 (registered in `lessons.json`). Tu BiShvat, Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av have no text or lesson in ALEF: the card shows none rather than a stand-in.

## 2. Festival vocabulary packs
Ten packs, 12 words each (Shavuot 11). Every word is a record of `data/vocab/words.json`, picked by id after reading its gloss; `vocab.json` is regenerated by `node src/festivals/build.mjs` and the test rebuilds it and requires the file to be identical, so no hand edits survive. Each reading line is the shortest line of `data/corpus/registry.json` or `phrasebook.json` that holds the word (exact spelling or one prefix letter), copied verbatim; 49 of the 119 words have one. No Hebrew was composed.

Gaps are named in the pack and in the test: the festival names themselves are mostly missing from the vocabulary (Hanukkah, Purim, Shavuot, sukkah, lulav, etrog, Haggadah, maror, megillah...), as are Haman, Esther, Moses, Egypt (Mitzrayim), dreidel, pomegranate. False friends not used: the entries spelled like Sinai ("Chinese") and Ruth ("roger"), and the one spelled like teshuvah ("answer"). Several picks have a gloss that is only close to the festival sense; they carry a note on screen (shofar is fine, "to sign" for Yom Kippur's sealing is not). Whether a word means the same in its reading line was not checked.

## 3. Shabbat-ready
Phrases from `data/festivals/shabbat-ready.json`: Candle blessing, Kiddush over wine (the Friday-night Kiddush), HaMotzi. Three, not five: **Shalom Aleichem and Birkat HaBanim are not in the corpus** (searched registry, phrasebook, service, curriculum, Torah-reading data and morning verses; the test fails if either appears, so it will prompt adding them). They are listed on screen as "Not in ALEF yet". Lecha Dodi (`lecha-dodi-1`) is in the registry and linked from the card but not in the checklist, because it was not on the requested list.

Coverage is `readableNow(...).lines` from the Progress lane (letters and signs taught to the learner): a phrase is "ready" when every line is fully decodable. That means decodable, not memorised or fluent, and the screen says so. If progress data cannot be read the phrases show with "Coverage unknown", never as ready.

## Not checked
- Rendering in a real browser: tests use the repo's fake DOM, not Chromium or a phone. No screenshot was taken.
- Accessibility beyond structure (Hebrew spans carry `lang="he" dir="rtl"`, links are real anchors, headings and a status footer exist); no screen-reader pass.
- Audio for the packs (none attached) and adding pack words to the review deck (the Theme packs screen does this; these packs do not).
- Reform versus other practice for which Shabbat portion to follow in the years the diaspora and Israeli schedules differ; the setting exists, the card follows it.
- That the Hebrew festival names shown (from `calendar.js`, unpointed Hebcal-style or pointed per the calendar notes) are the ones a given community uses.
