## Matching decision

The tray has no vowel marks, so a word is recognised by its **letters only**; on a match the pointed form, transliteration and English are shown. Final letters are folded to their base on both sides: placing מ at the end of שלום still builds the word, the screen writes ם and the status line says "At the end of a word, מ is written ם." Homographs that differ only in pointing (אָדָם / אָדֹם) keep the first by preference order; the second is dropped (293 such drops).

A placed string that is a word **and** the start of a longer word (אב → אבא) is not fired automatically: the status says it is a word and offers "Say it" or keep going. A string that no word continues is a dead end: the status names the nearest real word that shares the longest prefix and how many letters it shares. No hearts, no failure state.

## Progressive depth

- 0 words built: only the letters of the three suggested starter words (אַבָּא, אִמָּא, שָׁלוֹם → א ב ו ל מ ש, 6 tiles).
- Each word built opens 3 more letters (by frequency in the list) plus the letters of the words built and suggested; from 6 words built the whole alef-bet and a "final forms" row are open. "All letters" is one tap away at any time.
- Suggestions: unbuilt starters first (10 words of 2–4 letters), then unbuilt words whose level fits (level ≤ 1 + ⌊count/5⌋) and whose letters are open.
- First visit still reaches a first success in a handful of taps: splash → three choice screens → three tiles.

## Sound

On a match the view calls `ctx.audio.play('word', clip, { text })`: the player picks the learner's voice and tradition from Settings; the status line is `playLine()` from `src/pronunciation/forTradition.js` (the house helper), so it names what played. "Hear it slowly" calls the same clip with `rate: 'slow', noEcho: true` (0.7×, same voice, per `docs/audio/PLAYBACK-CONTRACT.md`); normal speed is the default. Letter clips play on each tile tap. Nothing falls through to a non-Hebrew voice.

## Tally and privacy

Store key `firstday.builder.v1` = `{ v, words: [ids], count, builds, lastAt }`, registered in `src/shell/privacy.view.js`. A learner whose first run is done (or who has built words) lands straight in the builder; `#/firstwin?replay=1` replays the splash and the three screens.

## Accessibility

Tiles are buttons (Tab/Enter); the word region is focusable and a keydown handler takes Hebrew letter keys (finals folded), Backspace, Enter (check), Escape (clear). The built word is announced through an `aria-live="polite"` region and the status line is `role="status"`. A letter that belongs to the chosen suggestion is marked by a thick underline and "in this word" in its aria-label, not by colour alone. The only motion is a 0.3 s transition on the built word, off under calm mode and `prefers-reduced-motion`; the sole haptic stays on the finish screen and is skipped in calm mode. No timer.
