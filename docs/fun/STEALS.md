# Ranked steals — Best App Starts (t1040u)

Research author’s ranking: each app is #1 at one thing; steal exactly that for Aleph Hebrew.

| Rank | App | #1 at | Steal for Aleph | Wired? |
|------|-----|-------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | **Duolingo** | Time-to-first-win + finish | Lesson before account; celebration chain; **one** completion haptic | Yes — TRY→SAVE; haptic on streak only |
| 2 | **Finch** | Emotional investment, no guilt | Name/create something early that learning feeds; missed day never shames | Yes — Day-1 “seder is lit / place stays held” |
| 3 | **Drops** | Visual, zero-friction start | Tap/drag only, no typing; illustrated script teaching | Partial — tray taps; full illustrated aleph-bet later |
| 4 | **Calm** | Splash + reflective finish | Open with hear+see **שָׁלוֹם** before any question; quote/verse share card | Yes — splash assemble + share card |
| 5 | **Busuu** | Concrete commitment | “At 5 min/day you’ll [target] by **[live date]**” | Yes — pre-lesson commitment screen |
| 6 | **Babbel** | Permission priming | Benefit screen at moment of need; never cold OS prompts | Deferred — ask after Save / Say |
| 7 | **Pimsleur** | Day-one conversation | End lesson 1 having *said* something real | Yes — optional Say beat |
| 8 | **Brilliant** | Respecting intelligence | Manipulate letters — build אבא from א+ב+א | Yes — Minute One builder |
| 9 | **Memrise** | Real-human authenticity | Native audio for every core phrase | Partial — production WaveNet/Chirp tracks (frozen) |
| 10 | **Fabulous** | Identity & pre-commitment | Future-self letter / optional sign-to-commit — **not** its 42 steps | Not yet — skip length |
| 11 | **Chess.com** | Protecting beginners | No scores/leaderboards/rank in week 1; best reading first | Yes — words LARGE before % |
| 12 | **Headspace** | Beginner routing | One “Start here” path; hide full library until lesson 3+ | Yes — progressive depth + motivation path |
| 13 | **Peak** | One legible number | Single reading score + in-lesson adapt | Later — not in Minute One |

**Cautionary (do not copy):** LingQ / Anki / Rosetta Stone blank-slate or immersion-first starts without scaffolding.

**Hebrew gap this owns:** Duolingo Hebrew has no formal aleph-bet/niqqud intro; HebrewPod101 walls behind email; Nemo is phrases. Aleph owns minute one with build + fade + reverence.
