# Dialect audition pack - blind judging

Each tradition folder has: blind/ (the audition candidate, code-named), control/ (the same items in the standard Israeli voice), key/ (the answer key - do NOT open until scoring is done).

Score every blind clip 1-10 on: (1) authenticity to the tradition, (2) clarity of the Hebrew sounds, (3) would you let a learner imitate this?
Gate (owner's casting rule): a slot passes only at authenticity >= 8 from at least 2 judges with no dimension under 6. Anything less stays UNFILLED and is never shipped as that tradition's voice.

These clips are synthetic approximations made with non-Hebrew voices reading transliterations. They are audition candidates only.
