# Hebrew lint: fixes and open items

Scope: the definite-error findings in `data/vocab` (and the other data files the lint reports). Passing the linter is not proof of correct Hebrew; only code-point shape was checked, and no word was changed into a different word.

## Applied (mechanical, via `data/vocab/corrections.json`)

| Record | Field | Old | New | Why safe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| he0fc0a82 (מֶזֶג) | forms.plural | מְְזָגִים | מְזָגִים | doubled sheva on the mem reduced to one; letters and other marks identical |

`tools/vocab/apply_corrections.mjs` was extended (backward compatible) so `field` may be a dotted path such as `forms.plural`. Copies in words.json, the pack file and the theme file were updated; themes, frequency, deck-order, attribution, pronunciation and audio builders were re-run and changed nothing else. `data/vocab/corrections.json` now itself contains the old doubled-sheva string (as the required `old` value), so the lint baseline gained that one archival fingerprint.

## Needs a human (vocab records, definite errors left unchanged)

Each value also appears in the pack file, the theme file, and where noted frequency.json, index.json, vocab5000.json, the vocab5k tier file and pronunciation shards (the corrections mechanism cannot reach those, so a fix needs a rebuild path, not only a corrections entry).

1. h448338e7, `he` = "טִיֵַּח" (tsere and patah both on the yod, patah before the dagesh). Looks like a furtive patah placed on the wrong letter; the forms (מְטַיֵּחַ, יְטַיֵּחַ) show the intended ending. Unsure because the intended order must be confirmed. Options: (a) correct to the pointing used by its own forms with a furtive patah under the final ח; (b) keep and mark as known defect. Also appears in frequency.json, index.json, vocab5000.json, vocab5k-tier-5000.json and pronunciation derived/runtime/variants.
2. heb3f471a, `example.he` "בֹּנֶה צִיּוֹן בְּדָמִים וִירוּשָׁלִַם בְּעַוְלָה": on the lamed of וִירוּשָׁלִַם both hiriq and patah. This is the traditional spelling quirk (patah and a hiriq for the yod); a source-text citation, so do not normalise blindly. Options: check against the source verse (Micah 3:10 style text), or drop the stray patah/hiriq to match the lesson source.
3. h1da16296, `forms.plural` "קַשְֹקַשִֹּים": shin carries sheva+holam and hiriq+holam, which is not valid pointing; the holam is probably a mistyped sin dot (U+05C2), but the vowels under it are unknown. Options: retype by hand from a reference (the singular is קַשְׂקֶשֶׂת), or remove the plural form.
4. h9167dfb6, `forms.plural` "מְקֻבִָּלים": bet has hiriq and qamats; the hiriq belongs on the lamed. Options: move the hiriq to the lamed (the word is a participle plural of מְקֻבָּל) after human confirmation, or drop the form.
5. h654755af, `forms.plural` "גּוּצִָים": tsadi has hiriq and qamats. One of the two vowels is spurious; which one is the author's intent is not recoverable. Options: keep hiriq (גּוּצִים) or qamats; confirm with a pointed source.
6. h48fc3422, `example.he` "אני זקוק לכ- 20 דקות...": non-final kaf before a hyphen. The abbreviation "לכ-" is conventional; likely a false positive. No change recommended; consider suppressing in the linter.

## Not fixed, by design

- `data/audio/manifest.json` entries 330 to 335 (שִׂין, sin dot before hiriq): generated by `tools/audio/build_manifest.mjs` from `tools/audio/hebrew_data.mjs`; fix at the source, then rebuild, not in the manifest.
- `data/corpus/registry.json` entries 43, 45, 46, 56: they record the wrong-order strings on purpose (before -> after correction notes).
- `data/photo/_fixtures/nakdan-recorded.json`: recorded external API responses; `data/photo/_benchmark/*`: deliberately malformed OCR; `data/corpus/source-snapshot.json`: source as received.
- Style findings (maqaf spacing, ASCII geresh/gershayim, plain pe at end) are conventions, not errors, and were not touched.
