# Aleph Gallery art: green and gold

Order sheet: `docs/images/gallery-art.csv` (6 rows: 1 header, 5 pictures). Nothing in this sheet has been drawn yet.

## Why

The Aleph Gallery page (Jewish art and life, 1800 to today) now uses the Aleph house theme: deep olive green, gold `#D9A441`, paper cream `#F6F1E7`, Heebo, Frank Ruhl Libre and Gveret Levin. Two places on that page still use placeholders: the four-panel Moses comic (emoji today) and the Reform service section (no picture today). This sheet orders one matching set for those slots, drawn in the same hand as the golden olive tree (`assets/img/gen/OPP_two_04`).

## Where to save

Save each picture under its exact name in this folder of the repo, then push:

```
assets/img/gallery/moses-01-staff.png
assets/img/gallery/moses-02-walls.png
assets/img/gallery/moses-03-chariots.png
assets/img/gallery/moses-04-miriam.png
assets/img/gallery/service-header.png
```

The page shows a picture when its file exists and keeps the current placeholder when it does not. After the push, the pictures are converted to WebP, checked for size and stripped of metadata before they are wired in.

## Palette

Deep green `#1C4023`, olive `#6B7F3A`, warm gold `#D9A441`, light gold `#E8C97A`, paper cream `#F6F1E7`, and for water only a muted navy `#2B3F6B` (the same navy as the olives on the golden tree). No other colours.

## Master command (paste once, then the 5 rows)

```
You are drawing a small matching set of pictures for a Hebrew learning app for adults.
House style: flat vector, thick rounded charcoal outlines, no gradients, no gloss, no photo-realism,
no cartoon sparkle. Match the look of a golden olive tree drawn in the same style: bold shapes,
warm gold trunks, deep green and olive leaves, cream background.

PALETTE for this set only: deep green #1C4023, olive #6B7F3A, warm gold #D9A441, light gold #E8C97A,
paper cream #F6F1E7, and muted navy #2B3F6B for water only. No other colours.

RULES FOR EVERY PICTURE
- No text of any kind: no letters in any alphabet, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no captions,
  no pseudo-Hebrew, no writing on scrolls, arks, walls or clothing. Captions are added by the page.
- No star of David, no flags, no logos, no halos, no depiction of God, no identifiable real people.
- Comic panels: 3:2 landscape, 1536 x 1024. Keep the top-left quarter calm and plain, because a
  caption box sits there. Keep the bottom-right corner calm, because a large Hebrew word sits there.
- The header: 3:2 landscape, 1536 x 1024, lower third kept quiet and dark green so a title can sit over it.
- Figures in simple robes in green, olive, gold and cream. Respectful, dignified, adult tone.

I will send the pictures one per line as: FILENAME | WHAT TO DRAW.
Reply with one image per line, saved under the exact FILENAME, then list the filenames you made.
```
