# Rimon: 200 Stills

Rimon is the pomegranate who keeps the learner company, the way the owl does in Duolingo. Besides the twelve animated moves (assets/img/rimon), these 200 still drawings let Rimon do something different in each part of the app: greet, cheer, comfort, celebrate festivals, train in the Brain Lab, read the little books, guard a streak and help when something goes wrong.

Built from `tools/images/rimon/stills.mjs` by `node tools/images/rimon/build.mjs`. Do not edit this file by hand.

## How a Still Gets Into the App

1. Make the picture from its prompt (full prompts: `docs/images/rimon-stills.csv` or `docs/images/rimon-stills.json`; one prompt per row, ready to paste). Attach the reference picture `assets/img/gen/MASCOT_rimon.800.webp` every time, and one cell of assets/img/rimon/wave.png (any one cell) when the tool takes a second picture, for size and position.
2. Save it with its file name from the order sheet, for example `rimon-007-starting-line.png`, in any drop folder.
3. Run `node tools/images/ingest_queue.mjs <drop folder>`. It checks the picture, makes it a 512 x 512 WebP with a transparent background, strips metadata, saves it as `data/images/rimon/v2/rimon-007-starting-line.webp` and updates `data/images/rimon/present.json`.
4. Commit both. The app shows a still only when its number is in `present.json`, so nothing changes on screen until the picture is really there.

A contact sheet of all 200 slots, with each prompt and a copy button, is at `docs/images/rimon-stills.html` (run `npm run serve`, then open `/docs/images/rimon-stills.html`). It shows which pictures exist and puts them side by side so off-model drawings stand out.

To check the app with the pictures in place, run `node tools/qa/qa-rimon-stills.mjs` (a real browser walks onboarding, Home, Brain Lab, an answer, a badge, page not found, a Torah screen, a phone and reduced motion; see the top of that file). `tools/qa/rimon-placeholders.py` makes numbered stand-in pictures for a dry run before any still is drawn.

## Rimon, On Model

Reference: `assets/img/gen/MASCOT_rimon.800.webp`. Colours sampled from it: body #DA2933, crown #D59E31, leaves #908B2A, sash #0A567C, cheeks #E95F67, eyes and mouth #633839.

Every prompt carries the same four blocks, word for word, so the 200 pictures stay one character:

- **Character.** Rimon is a cheerful pomegranate. Body: one round ball, very slightly wider at the bottom, flat pomegranate red #DA2933, no seeds showing, no shine, no texture. Crown: a short gold #D59E31 crown-shaped calyx sitting on top of its head, three rounded points, the middle point tallest (never five). Leaves: two olive-green #908B2A almond-shaped leaves tucked behind the crown, one angled out to each side. Face, in the upper half of the body: two small round dark maroon #633839 dot eyes set wide apart, a small thin curved maroon smile between them and a little lower, and two soft pink #E95F67 oval cheeks just below and outside the eyes. Sash: a deep blue #0A567C band wrapped around the middle of its body, tied at the front in a small knot with two short pointed tails hanging down. Limbs: thin red stick arms ending in small rounded mitten hands with four short fingers; two short red legs with small rounded feet; arms, legs and feet are the same red as the body. Rimon has no nose, no ears, no hair, no teeth and no clothes other than the sash and the props named below.
- **Style.** Flat 2D vector illustration exactly like the reference: solid flat colour fills, no outlines or strokes, no gradients, no highlights, no gloss, no texture, no grain, no 3D, no drop shadow, clean crisp edges. Expressions change only through the eyes (dots, happy upside-down arcs, closed curved lines, wide circles, a wink), small maroon eyebrow strokes only when this still asks for them, the mouth (small smile, open smile in dark maroon with a pink tongue, small round o, gentle frown) and the cheeks. Props use the same flat style and a small palette: the colours above plus cream #FAF2E0, white, warm brown #8A5A2B, soft grey #9AA3A0, sky blue #7FB8D6, dark green #1E4634 and gold.
- **Framing and output.** Square canvas, 1024 x 1024 pixels, fully transparent background (alpha). Rimon alone, full body, facing the viewer or turned a little to one side, centred left to right. Without props Rimon is about 80% of the canvas tall: the top of the crown about 8% below the top edge and the soles of its feet about 10% above the bottom edge, the same size and place in every still, so one still can replace another without a jump. Sitting or lying poses rest their lowest point on that same line. Props stay completely inside the canvas with a small margin; if a prop needs room, make Rimon a little smaller (never under 65% of the canvas height) and keep its feet on the same line. No scenery, no floor or ground line, no cast shadow, no vignette, no border or frame, no sparkles or glow unless this still names them.
- **Never.** No text of any kind: no letters in any alphabet (no Hebrew, no English), no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature. Anything that could carry writing (a sign, card, page, screen, flag, tile, badge, map or tablet) stays blank. Do not redesign Rimon: keep the round red body, the three-point gold crown, the two olive leaves, the dot eyes, the pink cheeks, the blue knotted sash and the thin red limbs exactly as in the reference. No other characters, no people.

Festival, Shabbat and synagogue stills add: Respect: Rimon never wears a prayer shawl, tefillin or a kippah, never holds a Torah scroll or a prayer book, and nothing in the picture shows the divine name or any Hebrew writing.

### Checklist Before a Still Is Saved

- Crown: gold, three rounded points (never five), middle one tallest; two olive leaves behind it, one each side.
- Face: two dot eyes (or the expression the row asks for), small maroon mouth, two pink oval cheeks. No nose, no teeth, no eyebrows unless asked.
- Sash: deep blue, tied at the front, knot and two short tails.
- Limbs: thin red arms, mitten hands with four fingers, short red legs, round feet.
- Flat colour, no outlines, no gradients or shine; transparent background; no floor or shadow.
- Same size and place as the others (crown near the top, feet on the same line near the bottom).
- No writing anywhere, in any alphabet. No people. Festival props drawn correctly (the chanukiah has nine candles: eight in a row and one raised).

## Where Rimon Steps Aside

- **Days of mourning and remembrance:** yom-hashoah, yom-hazikaron, tisha-bav. Rimon (the animated companion and every still) stays out of sight all day. To change this, edit QUIET_DAYS in src/shell/rimon-triggers.js.
- **Prayer, Torah and service screens:** #/torahread, #/torahreadsec, #/torahreader, #/service, #/trope, #/listen-shelf, #/morning, #/text, #/ask. The text has the screen to itself.
- **Shabbat:** the Shabbat stills never show Rimon lighting, cooking, writing or using a screen; the candles are already lit. Yom Kippur stills have no food or party.
- **Divine name:** no still contains writing, so none can show it. Spoken lines avoid it too.

## How Each Still Is Shown

- **pose:** Rimon in the corner (or the Home hero box) shows the still for a moment, then goes back to the animation.
- **page:** Rimon in the corner wears the still for the whole screen: dressed for that page.
- **hero:** A large Rimon inside the page, under the heading.
- **swap:** An existing picture of Rimon in that screen is replaced by the still.
- **toast:** A small card at the bottom of the screen with the still and one line; it goes away by itself.

Only one Rimon is on screen at a time: a large Rimon in the page, a card at the bottom, or a swapped Rimon picture inside the page each send the corner Rimon away until they are gone. So does a Rimon drawn into the page by another screen (the animated in-page Rimon, the Brain Lab pictures with Rimon in them: IN_PAGE_RIMON in src/shell/rimon-stage.js), which also keeps the big-moment and floating Rimon away. A still the learner has already seen once (first answer, milestones) is not shown again, and each daily greeting comes once a day. Hide Rimon hides every still too.

Hebrew in the lines below is pointed and has not yet been checked by a Hebrew teacher. Every line Rimon can say, in both languages, is in `docs/images/rimon-lines-review.csv` with empty checked_by, ok and fix columns for the reviewer; the build keeps those columns for any line whose words have not changed.
