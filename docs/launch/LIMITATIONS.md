# Known limitations (plain-English text for the About screen)

Status: draft text for the main lane to wire into the About screen. Written 2026-11-04. Each line says only what the repo showed on that date; update it as things change.

## What to know before you rely on ALEF

**ALEF is an early draft.** It is free and it works, but it has not been checked by the people who should check it.

**The Hebrew has not been reviewed.** The lessons, word lists, vowel points, transliterations, prayer texts and tradition comparisons were put together by programs. No native speaker, Hebrew teacher, rabbi or cantor has reviewed them yet. There will be mistakes. If something looks wrong, trust a printed siddur, a dictionary or your teacher over ALEF, and please tell us.

**There are no recordings yet.** Sound uses your device's built-in Hebrew voice, if it has one. That is a computer voice, not a teacher. Some phones and browsers have no Hebrew voice, and the ones that do vary in quality. Without one, you will see text but hear nothing.

**Speaking practice is limited.** It relies on your browser's speech recognition, which may not support Hebrew, and nobody has checked how well it understands learners. Treat any score as a rough hint.

**Traditions are approximate.** The Ashkenazi, Sephardi and Israeli readings are produced by spelling rules. Real speakers vary within each tradition, and no one has checked these against real speakers.

**Cantillation (trope) is only partly covered.** Only the Shema and the Priestly Blessing have marked-up readings so far.

**Word lists are machine-built.** Vocabulary comes from public word lists and is sorted by how often a word appears, not by what is best to learn first. Expect odd choices and some wrong meanings.

**It has not been tested on real phones.** It has been checked on a desktop browser pretending to be a phone, not on actual devices, and no screen reader user has tested it. Some screens may not fit small phones well, and a few buttons may be smaller than recommended.

**Your progress lives on this device only.** There is no account and no cloud copy. Clearing your browser data, or switching phones, erases it unless you export a backup in Settings.

**Offline use needs one good load first.** Open the app online once and let it finish loading. Some parts (photo reading, large word packs) download only when first used.

**Licences are still being sorted out.** Some word data and prayer texts come from public sources with their own licences. This has not been reviewed by a lawyer. See Credits for sources.
