# Coder 100: Games and Books Lane (12 November 2026)

Ideas D1 to D10 and E1 to E10 from the brief "ALEF — 100 coder-only ideas". Every item below is code and data the app already
had. The tests check logic and wiring in a fake DOM. They do not check Hebrew, glosses, the calendar table or audio quality, and no learner
has used these screens yet.

This lane changed that line to `extras.line(p)` in `src/books/book.view.js`, so new-word marks and karaoke highlighting both wrap the words of the line. When the two are merged, one function should draw the words and apply both.
- `data/books/words.json` and `data/torahstudy/search.json` are generated by `node tools/books/build-book-words.mjs` and `node tools/torahstudy/build-search.mjs`. Tests fail when they are stale.
- Pass-and-play and tournament boards hold no names or accounts. The tournament board shows only this device's runs.
