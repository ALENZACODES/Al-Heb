# B: nikkud-fade engine (feature a)

Status: PREVIEW tier, DRAFT. Route `#/nikkud-fade`. Gameplan pillar 1 ("full points, then partial, then none").

## What is real (built and tested)
- `src/nikkudfade/model.js`: pure engine. Stages 1 (full points), 2 (points only on new words and on look-alike words), 3 (no points, tap to reveal). Per-learner state in the store under `nikkudfade.v1`: stage, auto flag, per-word seen/aided counts, a window of recent reads, a log of stage changes.
- Schedule: auto mode advances a stage when the last `window` reads (10) are at most 20% aided and at least 10 reads happened in the stage; it demotes when at least 60% are aided. A learner can pin a stage by hand, which turns auto off. Numbers are in `data/nikkudfade/schedule.json`. They are design choices, not research results, and have not been tuned with learners.
- `data/nikkudfade/reading-set.json`: 240 common modern words, generated by `node tools/nikkudfade/build.mjs` from `data/vocab/words.json`. Pointed forms, transliteration and glosses are copied, nothing is hand-written. `ambiguous` = another entry among the 5,000 most frequent modern words has the same unpointed spelling and a different pointed form (100 of 240 are flagged; many are verb forms that differ only in binyan, so the flag is generous).
- The engine only removes points from stored forms; it never adds or guesses any.
- `src/nikkudfade/nikkudfade.view.js`: a round of 8 words; "Show the points" reveals (recorded as aided); "I read it" / "Not yet" are self-reports. Labelled controls, 44px minimum size, no animation, status region.
- Tests: `tests/nikkudfade.test.js` (13): stripping, stage display, schedule up/down, manual pin, junk state, session picking, data derived from words.json, view behaviour in the fake DOM.

## What is not real / limits
- "Read" is self-reported. Nothing is machine-graded.
- Not yet wired into existing reading views or exercises (lessons, vocab, Torah reader). Integration point for those: load `nikkudfade.v1` with `normalizeState`, call `display(state, {id, he, ambiguous}, {revealed})` per word, and `recordRead` when the learner finishes a word. Only vocabulary words with an id and a pointed form can be faded; free sentences (Tatoeba examples are unpointed in the data) cannot.
- Transliteration is shown only after answering; the gameplan's "slow plus per-word audio for every sentence" is not built here (no audio ids for these words were checked).
- Browser rendering at 375x667 was not run (no browser harness used); the 44px size is set inline and checked in the fake DOM only.
- Needs native review: the stage wording, and whether the 240-word set suits a beginner order.
- `views.generated.js` was not regenerated (precache build is done at push time); the route is non-optional so it is reachable meanwhile. `tests/integration.test.js` (VIEW_FILES check) will need that regeneration.

## Update after R/M audit (same session)
- Look-alike rule tightened (R audit, main issue 2): a rival must be among the 1,500 most frequent modern words; 66 of 240 items flagged (was 100). The count is written into the data note and pinned by a test. Items whose gloss is over 40 characters are skipped (no more "used to introduce a semantically definite direct object" card).
- Checkable step (R-010): for a flagged look-alike the learner picks which sound they read (own sound or the rival's, from `rival` in the data); a wrong pick is stored as aided. Plain words still use the self-report path. This is a check on the learner's choice, not on their voice.
- Lesson-aware sessions (R audit, main issue 3): sessions use words whose lesson number is within the learner's progress (`curriculum.progress`), minimum lesson 10 (58 of the 240 words); a toggle widens to every lesson.
- Lookup for other screens (R-008, M-R-008): `data/nikkudfade/homographs.json` (713 vocabulary rows that have a look-alike within the cap) and `ambiguousFor(homographs, id)`. Not yet read by `review.view.js` (R-007 / M-R-007 is a v0-screen edit; not done).
- Links (R-009): the page links to the Torah-text ladder (`#/torahread/ladder`) and the 20-step ladder (`#/firstday/ladder`).
- `rng` now imported from `src/curriculum/spotit.js` (same algorithm); `stripPoints` agrees with `heUnpointed` on all 6,303 words except 13 prefix entries that differ only by the maqaf (tested).
