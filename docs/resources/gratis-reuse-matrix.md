## Summary

- Openly licensed for reuse: only Wikibooks (CC BY-SA, confirmed in the footer). FSI is claimed public domain by a mirror and is the other candidate; DLI is unverified.
- Share-alike: copying any Wikibooks text makes that adapted part CC BY-SA. If ALEF ships a paid book or app with closed licensing, avoid copying and write original content. If ALEF is open, it must attribute (title, authors via page history link, licence link) and mark changes.
- Non-commercial or personal use only: Learn101, LAITS (non-commercial, credit). These rule out copying into a sold product.
- No licence stated (default all rights reserved): TeachMeHebrew, A Little Hebrew, StoryOlive, Jewish Miami, DLI, the YouTube channel, and the user's sheet.
- Facts such as a word's meaning are not protected by themselves, but selection, ordering and wording of a compiled list can be. ALEF should assemble its own lists from a source whose licence it can cite.
- Frequency-list sources outside this directory (for example the hermitdave list already in research notes) were not assessed here; check their licence before use.

## Before shipping anything derived

1. Record the source and licence in the corpus registry for every text.
2. Do not copy audio from any source here; ALEF records its own or uses declared TTS.
3. Native-speaker review for anything written by ALEF in Hebrew.
