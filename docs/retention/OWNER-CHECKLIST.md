# Retention: Twenty Minutes on a Real Phone

Nothing in this folder has been tried on a phone. This list covers what the automated checks could not. Use the review site after deploying the branch, or run `npm run serve` and open it from the phone on the same network.

## Before You Start

- Use a browser profile with no ALEF data, or clear the site's data.
- Android with Chrome shows the most. iPhone covers the calendar fallback.

## The Walk

| # | Do this | You should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open ALEF for the first time. | No streak, gems or hearts in the top bar, and no goal card. The first screen is as it was. |
| 2 | Finish the first lesson. | A reward panel: "+10 XP", "1-day streak!", "+50 gems". Then a card: "Want a nudge from Rimon?" |
| 3 | Tap "Yes, remind me". | The phone's own permission question. After "Allow", a line saying reminders are on. |
| 4 | Go Home. | Top bar with flame, gems and hearts. A "Daily XP Goal" card with a ring, seven day dots and a "3-minute lesson" button. |
| 5 | Open the Shop from the top bar. Tap "Send a test reminder". | A notification titled "Rimon" with the gold Aleph, not Rimon, as its icon. Tap it: ALEF opens. |
| 6 | Finish a second lesson. | "New for You: Daily quests are open." Home now has "Today's Quests". |
| 7 | Tap "3-minute lesson" on a longer lesson and go through four parts. | "Three Minutes Done" at the top, with "Keep going" and "Done for today". |
| 8 | Finish a third lesson, then answer a question wrong on purpose. | "Hearts are on" after the third lesson. The heart count in the top bar drops by one. Answer the same question wrong again: it does not drop. |
| 9 | In the Shop, buy a streak freeze if you have 200 gems. | One question, then "Freeze ready." |
| 10 | Turn on calm mode in Accessibility. | The top bar, goal card and quests all disappear. Turn it off again. |
| 11 | On Friday after 6 pm, or on Saturday, open Home. | "Shabbat Shalom. Your streak rests today too." No banner asking you to study. On Saturday, "No quests today." |
| 12 | iPhone, in Safari (not installed): finish a first lesson. | The card offers "How to add" and "Add to calendar" instead of the permission question. The calendar file opens with a daily event, Sunday to Thursday. |
| 13 | Android, installed to the Home Screen, reminders on: do not open ALEF for two days. | A reminder may arrive around the time you usually study. If none arrives in three days, that is the browser's choice; note it. |
| 14 | Reach a 3-day streak. | A "Share your streak" button. It should open the phone's share sheet. |
