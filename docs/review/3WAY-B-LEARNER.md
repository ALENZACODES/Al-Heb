# Reviewer B: learner experience and product review

Lens: would a total beginner enjoy the first session and come back? Reviewed 2026-11-05 on the `main` checkout (the commit with the "ranked list of the next clips" audio change at the tip).

## How this was done (and what that means)

- I drove the app myself in headless Chromium (Playwright from `/opt/npm-tools`, the repo's own `tools/serve.mjs`) with small throwaway scripts kept outside the repo. Phone viewport 390x844 for most runs; 360x640 with dark mode and the largest text size for one run.
- Fresh profile: welcome screens, 3 onboarding steps, "Your first 10 minutes" step 1, the lesson list, lesson r01 start to finish (5 quick-check questions, a deliberate wrong first pass and a correct pass), r02 and r03 read through, letter tracing (a wrong drag), Home, Progress, Settings, `/daily`, Install, Offline, About, one Israel dialogue, the "speak in Israel + I can read vowels" onboarding branch.
- Returning learner: a profile seeded with 8 completed lessons (`alef:curriculum.progress`). Caveat: seeding marks lessons done without creating review cards, so "No reviews due" on that profile is an artefact of my seed and says nothing about real review behaviour.
- I looked at screenshots with the image reader. 18 are committed in `docs/review/3WAY-B-shots/` (jpeg, 640 KB total).
- Everything below is "what I saw in headless Chromium". It is scripted use by me, not a learner test.

## Score: would a beginner stay past day 3? 45 / 100

This is my judgment, anchored to the rubric below. It is not measured retention and no real learner was involved.

| Part | Max | Mine | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| First 5 minutes: clarity of what to do and why | 15 | 8 | 3 setup screens before any Hebrew; first-day card is clear once reached, but the warning text and sound banner crowd it |
| Teaching quality of lessons 1 to 3 | 20 | 11 | Sound letter-by-letter order, good memory hooks, good wrong-answer explanations; but lessons are read-then-quiz pages, not guided practice, and lesson 1 carries 5 new signs |
| Motivation loop (streak, rewards, goals, return reason) | 20 | 5 | The loops exist (`/daily`, badges, celebration) but none of it fires on the beginner path; no streak, XP or celebration on Home or lesson finish |
| Audio experience | 15 | 3 | Clips exist for 1 of 22 words in lessons r01 to r10 and for no letters; everything else is device voice that headless cannot judge |
| Visual polish and pictures | 10 | 5 | Tracing is genuinely good and the picture hooks are charming; the picture is a 50px thumbnail, page chrome is text-heavy |
| Trust and accessibility | 10 | 6 | Very honest copy, a11y groundwork and 48px targets are real; honesty copy is too loud and has two inconsistencies |
| Load speed and offline | 10 | 7 | Fast local load, precache works in my offline reload; the offline messaging is alarming |

Rubric for the 0 to 100 number: 100 means a first session where the learner hears Hebrew, gets a win and a reason to return within 5 minutes, and nothing makes them doubt the app. Below 40 means the learner hits a wall (no sound, no feedback, confusing steps) in the first session. 45 is "the bones of a good course, with the three things that keep people (sound, wins, a tight first lesson) not yet reachable on the default path".

## Ranked findings (top 25)

Effort: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more (including content work that needs people).

1. **No recorded or clip audio on the default path; clips do not cover the first lessons.** Where: `data/audio/manifest*.json`, `audio/m/israeli/word/`. A learner on the default settings (voice Woman, style Sephardi, per `src/shell/settings.js`) gets no clip at all. Even with Man + Israeli, I counted by script 22 words in lessons r01 to r10: 1 has a clip; 0 of 27 letters have one. All 87 clips are single words from vocab and later lessons. What a learner sees: tap Listen, hear the phone's voice (or, as in my headless runs, "No sound: the browser voice reported an error"). Fix: before anything else record or generate (with the review gate you already have) the 27 letter names, the 5 first vowels, and the r01 to r10 words, for the default voice and style. Effort L (content), S to wire.
2. **Nothing celebrates a finished beginner lesson.** Where: `src/curriculum/learn.view.js` completion, versus `preview/loops/hook.js`, which only `src/curriculum/intermediate.view.js:371` calls. What a learner sees: a plain "Lesson complete / 5 of 5 right first time / Accuracy 100% / Time 0:40" panel. No streak, no points, no badge, no confetti, even though all of those exist in `preview/loops`. Fix: call `lessonFinished` from the r01 to r10 completion and put today's streak/goal ring on Home. Effort M.
3. **No streak or daily-goal ring on Home; the motivation layer lives only at `/daily`, marked Preview.** Where: `src/shell/router.js` line for `daily` (tier preview), Home page. What a learner sees: Home shows "Daily goal: 5 min" in a collapsed row and "0 of 20 points" only if they find `/daily`. A streak line appears only after streak rules are met. Fix: promote a minimal streak + goal ring to Home. Effort M.
4. **Right and wrong feedback in "Your first 10 minutes" is easy to miss.** Where: `src/firstday/firstday.view.js` quick check. What a learner sees: the answer buttons do not change colour or get a tick; the verdict ("Yes." or "Not quite: it is Shin. Nothing is lost.") is a plain line below the fold under "Why?". I had to scroll to see it (screenshot `05-firstday-feedback-below-fold.jpg`). The lesson quick check does it well (tick on the chosen button, "Right." beside it, screenshot `09-lesson-quickcheck.jpg`). Fix: reuse the lesson component's markup here. Effort S.
5. **The first-day quick check answers itself.** Where: first-day step 1. The heading already says "Your first letter: Shin" and shows the letter, then asks "Which letter is this? Shin / Samekh / Lamed", then "Which letter sounds like: sh with the right dot, s with the left dot" (no audio, and dots are not explained yet). Zero retrieval effort, so the "Win 1 of 5" is not earned. Fix: ask after the learner has traced; hide the name above the question; use the hook picture as a distractor-free cue. Effort S.
6. **Three setup screens and a 7-day plan before the first Hebrew letter.** Where: `/welcome` flow. Screen 1 (goal, reading level), screen 2 (Sephardi/Ashkenazi/Israeli plus a "Start without sound" checkbox), screen 3 (a 7-day plan with minutes). Then a "Start" button. A true beginner cannot meaningfully choose a pronunciation tradition. Fix: default to one style, defer the choice to the first time the learner hears a difference, and drop straight into letter 1 after one question. Effort M.
7. **Plan minutes contradict the 10-minute goal.** Where: welcome screen 3. "Your goal is 10 minutes a day. The lessons below add up to about 155 minutes this week", Day 1 (30 min), lessons of 20 to 25 min. In my run the whole r01 quick check took 40 seconds of active time; reading the page is not counted. A learner told "10 minutes a day" and shown 30 minutes on day 1 will feel behind immediately. Fix: show chunks (the app already says "5 steps, about 2 minutes each") and show the day as 10 minutes of the first chunks. Effort S.
8. **Lessons read as long pages, not as a guided sequence.** Where: `/learn?lesson=r01` is 2,521 px tall: letter cards, 6 collapsed "How X is said, in words" sections, "Why? Remember it", "Optional: Spot It drills", a vowels block, a words block, then "Start quick check" at the very end (screenshots `15-lesson1-top.jpg`, `16-lesson1-rest.jpg`). The button "Start with one letter" near the top is the only guided path and it is not obvious that it is the main route. Duolingo-style one-exercise-at-a-time (from memory, not rechecked) is the benchmark here. Fix: make "Start with one letter" the default, collapse the reference sections. Effort M.
9. **Lesson 1 carries five new things with weak differentiation.** Shin, Bet, Kamatz, Patach and the shin dot, plus a verb ("shav, he returned"). Kamatz and Patach are both described as "a as in father" with no comparison shown, and the app shows no side-by-side. A beginner cannot tell why there are two. Fix: teach one vowel per lesson at first, and say "same sound here; the marks differ" in one line. Effort S for copy, M to re-split.
10. **A yellow sound banner appears on every first load and the very first screen says "Sound: the list has not been loaded yet."** Where: `#sound-status` in `src/shell/audio-status.js` family, screenshot `01-welcome-sound-banner.jpg`. It is a warning-coloured strip with "Full message", "Details" and an X on the welcome screen, before the learner has asked for sound. In offline mode it becomes "the list of recordings could not be loaded (offline?)". Fix: stay silent until the learner taps a sound control, then show one short line. Effort S.
11. **Honest-copy overload crowds the first screens.** Where: first-day page: "Draft, unreviewed: no Hebrew teacher has checked it and no learner has tried it yet", then "Reading style: Sephardi. No Sephardi audio yet. You chose to hear the device voice anyway: it speaks Israeli Hebrew...", then a third audio paragraph below the quiz. The honesty is good policy; the volume and placement (above the content) is not. Also, the learner did not "choose" the device voice: it is the default (`browserVoiceAnyway: true`). Fix: one dismissible "early version" chip linking to About; fix the wording. Effort S.
12. **About says "Pictures: none approved yet, so no generated picture is shown", but pictures are shown.** Where: `/about` ("What is ready, and what is not") against the shortcake picture on first day, lesson page and letter pages (README says 9 of 28 are installed). A claim that does not match what is on screen is the kind of trust slip this app works hard to avoid. Fix: update the sentence (for example "28 memory pictures are drafts, self-rated, not reviewed"). Effort S.
13. **Settings: the voice choice does nothing yet, and defaults point away from the only clips.** Where: `/settings`. Text: "The Man and Woman choice is saved for later. It does not change the sound yet." Default voice is Woman (Noa); the only clips are Man (Ari), Israeli. Settings opens with about 4 paragraphs of status text before the first control (screenshot `12-settings-top.jpg`). Fix: hide Man/Woman until it works; move status text under "What is ready". Effort S.
14. **Home is sparse and half the tiles are "Preview".** Where: Home (screenshots `06-home-fresh.jpg`, `18-returning-home.jpg`). A fresh learner gets four small tiles (Letters, Words Preview, Read Preview, Progress) and one card. The returning home adds a backup-reminder card larger than the lesson card. No sense of "path", level or what comes next. Fix: show the next 3 lessons as a visible path and move the backup nudge to a slim banner. Effort M.
15. **Lesson list is a flat list of 71 lessons with "Mark complete" beside "Start".** Where: `/learn` (screenshot `07-learn-list.jpg`). No path graphic, no unit headers beyond a small "WHITE BELT" chip. "Mark complete" on lesson 1 lets a learner skip the work and inflate progress; the Progress page then counts it as practised (my seeded 8 lessons showed "9 of 27 letters, 7 of 15 signs"). Fix: a path view with units, and put "Mark complete" behind a "I already know this" menu. Effort M.
16. **Completion panel is noisy and has two contradictions.** Where: end of the r01 check (screenshot `08-lesson-complete.jpg`). The "Answered right" line renders as `ש Shin, ש, ב Bet, ב, שָׁב he returned` (letters repeated, bidi order scrambled), and it offers both "Review now" and "Nothing to review." Fix: show one row per item, hide "Review now" when nothing is due. Effort S.
17. **Quiz counter grows when you miss ("Question 2 of 6", "3 of 7", then "6 of 10").** A learner who gets the first answers wrong watches the total grow, which reads as punishment even though the app says "Nothing is lost". Fix: keep "5 questions" and show "2 to redo" separately. Effort S.
18. **Pictures are a 50px thumbnail behind a blue "Picture, sound, word" button.** Where: first-day step 1, letter pages. The charming picture-to-letter idea (a shortcake whose candles are the shin) is the best mnemonic in the app, yet the default view hides it. Duolingo and similar apps put the illustration in the lesson itself (from memory). Fix: show the picture at 120 to 160px by default on a letter's first appearance. Effort S. (Remaining unwired drafts are out of my lens: I did not review `docs/drafts`, which I deleted from my clone to save space.)
19. **Tracing works and feels good, but the feedback sits under the canvas.** Where: `/letters/shin`. I dragged a wrong stroke: it drew a red line and left the stroke count at 0, with the hint "Start at the green dot and finish at the arrow" below the canvas, out of sight on a phone (screenshot `14-trace-wrong-stroke.jpg`). Large canvas, numbered start dot, "Animate", "Hint", "Walk me through it with buttons" are all good. Fix: show the hint as an overlay on the canvas. Effort S.
20. **Review and spaced repetition are invisible until a lesson is finished.** Where: `/learn?review=1`: "Nothing due. All caught up." Home: "No reviews due." A learner on day 2 hears about review but has nothing to do with it. I could not test real due-card behaviour (see "could not check"). Fix: after lesson 1, schedule a 10-minute review for tomorrow and say so on the completion panel ("Come back tomorrow: 5 cards"). Effort S.
21. **Install and offline pages talk like a reviewer, not a learner.** Where: `/install` ("This browser has not offered a one-tap install"), `/offline` ("0 of 341 files are saved... it has not been tested in airplane mode", 10 groups listed by file counts). In my run, a service-worker profile did cache 341 shell files and reloaded `#/learn`, `?lesson=r01`, `/letters/shin` and `/progress` with the network off. Fix: one sentence, "Works offline: yes" with a single status. Effort S. The offline view says "not tested in airplane mode" while the repo has an offline QA script (`tools/qa/offline.mjs`) that I did not run; my own offline reload above is the only offline check I can vouch for.
22. **Largest text on a 360x640 phone leaves little room.** Where: any page with the sound banner and the 3-item bottom bar at the largest text size: banner plus header consume about 150 px at the top and the bar about 85 px at the bottom, leaving roughly 60 percent of the screen for content (screenshot `13-dark-largest-360.jpg`). Fix: let the banner scroll away; hide it once dismissed for the session. Effort S.
23. **Navigation is text-only.** Bottom bar: "Learn / Progress / More", no icons, no Home item (Home is the logo). Learners scanning for a home or a daily button have to know the logo is Home. Fix: icons plus a Home item. Effort S.
24. **Israel track starts in pointed Hebrew with no audio, and its richest content is Preview.** Where: onboarding "Speak in Israel + I can read with vowel marks" leads to "Hello, thanks, sorry" lesson; `/dialogues/d-cafe` is Preview-banner content with "There is no audio for them yet" (screenshot not taken). For a goal of "speak in Israel", a transliteration-first, listen-first path is the expected fit. Fix: a transliteration-on-by-default option for that track and an audio-first first lesson once clips exist. Effort M to L.
25. **Content and review gates are fine for trust but make the product feel unfinished to a first-time visitor.** Hebrew has had no native review, the clip licences are recorded as "unknown: not cleared" in the `.meta.json` files, and the About page says there is no software licence. These are right to disclose and I saw the disclosures. They also mean nothing here is shippable to strangers yet, which is a launch gate, not a polish item. Fix: keep the honesty, and gate public sharing on the review and licence items. Effort L (people and legal).

## Short notes by area (for the other reviewers to compare)

- **First 5 minutes:** welcome (2 questions) then pronunciation, then plan, then "Start". Time from open to first letter was 4 taps. Local load was 0.86 s to `data-ready` on my machine with no throttling.
- **Teaching order, lessons 1 to 3:** Shin and Bet; Tav and the Bet dagesh; Lamed, final Mem, Vav with Cholam and the word "shalom". Each letter has a hook line ("Three flames glow on a shortcake: sh!") and a plain-English sound description; wrong answers get a specific tip ("Bet's base sticks out to the right past the back..."). That is good teaching copy. Weak spots: r02 says "Tav says T... Tet sounds the same" before Tet is taught; "Dagesh: same" is cryptic; r01 to r10 have only 22 word items in total, so by lesson 10 a learner has met about two dozen words.
- **Practice feel:** lesson quick checks are multiple-choice only in what I saw (letter name, sound description, meaning). No audio-in, speaking, typing or matching inside the lesson flow itself. Those exist as separate Preview routes (`/speak`, `/listen`, `/typing`) I did not exercise.
- **Feedback wording:** warm and specific ("Not quite. The answer is Shin." then a tip; "Still tricky. It will come back in your review."). First-day wording is thinner ("Yes.").
- **Motivation:** the loops design (`/daily`) is the best-written part of the app: "Badges are earned, never lost, never bought and never compared with anyone", rest days, optional quests. It is just not connected to the beginner path (findings 2 and 3).
- **Progress page:** accurate-sounding but dense; rows like "0 of 129 lines, 0 words" and "Siddur coverage, Shabbat morning" mean little on day 1 (screenshot `11-progress-fresh.jpg`). A beginner wants "3 letters learned" and a bar.
- **Accessibility impressions (headless only):** skip link present; tab order on `/learn` goes straight to the track buttons then lesson buttons; dark mode readable; targets looked 48px; text size setting works. I did not use a screen reader, a real finger or reduced-motion. Not checked: contrast ratios beyond eyeballing.
- **Comparison with Duolingo Hebrew:** I can only compare against my own recollection of how Duolingo teaches (short exercises, audio on nearly every item, a streak and XP on the first screen, a visible path). I did not open Duolingo and I did not verify what its current Hebrew course contains, so I make no claim about its letter coverage or exercise types. On the areas where the Aleph app is ahead in my view: hooks per letter, tracing, free and offline, honesty about status, pronunciation-tradition awareness. Where it is behind: sound, wins, the first-session shape.

## What I could not check

- Real audio. Headless Chromium cannot play sound; the device voice reported an error in every run. I cannot say how the device voice sounds, whether a Hebrew voice exists on a given phone, or whether the 87 synthetic clips are correct Hebrew (no one has reviewed them, per the files themselves). I checked only which words have clips, by matching the clip texts against `data/curriculum/lessons.json` for r01 to r10.
- Real phones, fingers, tablets, iOS Safari, Android Chrome, screen readers, haptics.
- Installability as an actual PWA install (I read `manifest.webmanifest` and the install page; I did not trigger the browser's install prompt).
- Real spaced-repetition behaviour across days (my seeded profile had no review cards; I did not advance a clock).
- Day 2 to day 7 behaviour, streak rules and badges earned through real use. I saw only the empty `/daily` state and the code path that calls the celebration.
- The Preview routes beyond `/daily` and one dialogue (speak, listen, typing, read, Torah reader, trope, calendar).
- Hebrew correctness of any text. I am not a Hebrew reviewer.
- Slow-network feel. I ran one emulated "slow 4G, 4x CPU slowdown" cold load: about 0.8 MB over 62 requests and 5.8 s to ready, then about 2.8 MB after the service worker's precache. That is an emulation, not a field measurement.
- The unwired picture and animation drafts in `docs/drafts` (deleted from my clone to save space; I did not review them).
- Other reviewers' files: not read, by instruction.
