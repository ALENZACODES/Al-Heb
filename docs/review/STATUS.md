## What this does and does not cover

- Covered: word lists of the core and intermediate lessons, the 1000 most frequent vocabulary words, prayer and Torah registry lines, the trope table and two cantillated texts, pronunciation respellings per tradition, dialogues and speaking sets, festival vocabulary.
- Not covered: lesson titles, goals and exercise prompts, example sentences, other vocabulary beyond rank 1000, letter-stroke cards, the calendar, the book, audio (none exists). The older packet tooling in `docs/review/README.md` covers some of these.
- The pronunciation packs hold rule-generated respellings. Those rules and the three traditions were set by the project, not by a reviewer.
- Reviewer types: Hebrew teacher; Cantor or rabbi (liturgy and trope); Israeli native speaker (modern usage).

Workflow: build packs, send CSV (and the print HTML) with INSTRUCTIONS.md, then `node tools/review/ingest.mjs returned.csv --reviewer NAME --type teacher|cantor|native --date YYYY-MM-DD` (dry run), add `--write` to record. Ingest never edits content.
