# Listening guide: 87 short Hebrew clips

Thank you for listening. This takes about 10 to 20 seconds per clip. You can stop at any time and skip any clip you cannot judge.

## What you are hearing

- **A computer-made voice, not a person.** Every clip is one pointed Hebrew word, spoken by a synthetic voice. No one recorded them and no one has listened to them yet.
- A computer program checked the clips for length and loudness, and an automatic recognizer matched them to the written word. That proves very little. It cannot tell whether the vowels, the stress or the accent are right. **You are the first human check.**
- The label on each clip says which tradition it is meant for (all 87 say Israeli). Nobody has confirmed the voice really sounds that way. Please say what you hear.

## What to judge, for each clip

1. **Is the word correct Hebrew as it is pointed on the screen?** Spelling and vowel marks, as written.
2. **Vowels.** Does the voice say the vowels the pointing shows?
3. **Stress.** Is the stressed syllable the right one for this word?
4. **Clear.** Is it one clean word, not cut off, doubled, noisy or distorted?
5. **Voice.** Would you be comfortable with a learner hearing this voice as a model?
6. **Accent (only if you can tell).** Does it sound Israeli, Sephardi or Ashkenazi? "Not sure" is a fine answer.

## How to mark each clip

Choose one:

- **Approve**: correct and clear; you would let a learner hear it.
- **Approve with note**: acceptable, but say in the note what is slightly off.
- **Reject**: not acceptable. Tick what was wrong (word, vowels, stress, unclear, voice) and add a note if you can.
- **Cannot tell**: you could not judge it. This is a useful answer.

Leave a clip blank if you did not listen to it. Blank means "not reviewed", not "fine".

Please do not change the Hebrew or the id. If the word itself is wrong, write the right word in the note.

## Two ways to answer

- **The listening page** (`listen.html`, open it in any browser; no internet needed). Keep the `audio` folder next to it. Press play, pick a verdict, and at the end press **Export my answers**. Your answers are saved in your browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same computer.
- **The sheet** (`listen-sheet.csv`, opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). Play the file named in the `file` column and fill in the `verdict` column with exactly one of: `approve`, `approve-with-note`, `reject`, `cannot-tell`. Add your name, role and the date. Save as CSV (UTF-8), not .xlsx.

Send back the exported or saved CSV file.

## What happens to your answers

**Nothing counts as approved because you said so on this page.** Your answers are advice to the owner. A clip is only treated as approved once the owner records it, using the project's normal approval step, which is done by hand and one clip at a time. The owner may keep your name and the date with that record. If you reject or cannot tell, the clip stays unapproved.
