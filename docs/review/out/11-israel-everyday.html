israel-everyday:israel/giveaways:gv-noun-adjP4 · flagged: proofread, needsNativeReview
wrong: הַמִּסְעָדָה טוֹב
right: הַמִּסְעָדָה טוֹבָה
wrong: hamis'aDA tov
right: hamis'aDA toVA
The adjective does not match the noun
