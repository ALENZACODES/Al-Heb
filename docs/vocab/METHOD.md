# Vocabulary method (vocab5000.json)

Pipeline: `tools/vocab/fetch.py` (sources, git-ignored cache) -> `build_lexicon.py` -> `build_words.mjs` (lemmatised, ranked `words.json`, 6,029 modern/liturgical words) -> `build_vocab5k.py` (this deliverable) -> `coverage.py` (counts vs reference sheet).

1. Ranking. Earlier stages grouped OpenSubtitles surface forms under Wiktionary lemmas (inflections summed onto the lemma, homographs flagged) and ranked by `min(modernRank, 1.5 * readRank)`, so words frequent in the Tanakh/prayer registry are pulled up. This step keeps the top candidates, drops non-lemma glosses (alternative spellings, plurals, given names, surnames, letter names, symbols, onomatopoeia; 25 dropped), removes duplicate (form, POS) pairs (18), repairs 4 glosses that lost their content upstream, and re-ranks 1..5000.
2. Curated core. 77 entries are flagged `curatedCore`: days, months (Gregorian and Hebrew), seasons, colours, directions, time words and some numerals. 52 of them were not in the ranked pool and were added from Wiktionary with a fixed priority (`sourceRank: null`); the rest only had their topic overridden. Feminine numerals 1-10 are forms of the masculine numerals in Wiktionary, so they are not separate entries.
3. Topics (27). Assigned by heuristic: POS for Verbs, Adjectives, Pronouns, Numbers; Wiktionary categories, then gloss keywords for nouns; leftovers are Misc Nouns (1,157, 23%, mostly abstract nouns). Adverbs go to Time / Location / Directions when the gloss says so, else Adjectives; prepositions, conjunctions, particles and interjections without a spatial or temporal sense are filed under Misc Nouns (their `pos` field stays accurate). Religion and Jewish-life words are filed under Society (there is no Religion topic in the 27). The topic labels were spot-checked on samples, not exhaustively; expect some mis-filings (for example a few Society items that are household objects).
4. Tiers. `tier` is the introduction band: 500 (ranks 1-500), 1000 (501-1000), 2000 (1001-2000), 5000 (2001-5000). `src/vocab/vocab.js` has cumulative helpers.
5. Rebuild: `python3 -I tools/vocab/build_vocab5k.py` (needs the cache from `fetch.py`), then `node --test`.

Known weaknesses: some Wiktionary first senses are rare ones (a few glosses are wrong for modern use, for example a promise glossed as "belt"); some tail entries are loanwords or slang; `readList` is true when a word is in the Tanakh/prayer read list (readRank <= 3100), not a measure of how much it was boosted.
