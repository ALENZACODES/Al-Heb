# Vocabulary method (vocab5000.json)

Pipeline: `tools/vocab/fetch.py` (sources, git-ignored cache) -> `build_lexicon.py` -> `build_words.mjs` (lemmatised, ranked `words.json`, 6,029 modern/liturgical words) -> `build_vocab5k.py` (this deliverable; imports `refine.py` and reads `data/vocab/overrides.json`) -> `quality.py` (suspicious-item counts, before/after) and `coverage.py` (counts vs reference sheet).

## Round 1 (base build)

1. Ranking. Earlier stages grouped OpenSubtitles surface forms under Wiktionary lemmas (inflections summed onto the lemma, homographs flagged) and ranked by `min(modernRank, 1.5 * readRank)`, so words frequent in the Tanakh/prayer registry are pulled up. The base build keeps the top candidates, drops non-lemma glosses (alternative spellings, plurals, given names, surnames, letter names, symbols, onomatopoeia; 25 dropped), removes duplicate (form, POS) pairs (18), repairs 4 glosses that lost their content upstream, and re-ranks 1..5000.
2. Curated core. 77 entries are flagged `curatedCore`: days, months (Gregorian and Hebrew), seasons, colours, directions, time words and some numerals. 52 of them were not in the ranked pool and were added from Wiktionary with a fixed priority (`sourceRank: null`). Feminine numerals 1-10 are forms of the masculine numerals in Wiktionary, so they are not separate entries (they now appear in `forms[]`, see below).
3. Topics (27, unchanged). Assigned by heuristic: POS for Verbs, Adjectives, Pronouns, Numbers; Wiktionary categories, then gloss keywords for nouns; leftovers are Misc Nouns.
4. Tiers. `tier` is the introduction band: 500 (ranks 1-500), 1000 (501-1000), 2000 (1001-2000), 5000 (2001-5000). `src/vocab/vocab.js` has cumulative helpers.

## Round 2 (quality pass, 2026-11-03)

Triggered by defects the UI review found (for example `in` and `to` under Misc Nouns, `no` typed as an adverb, a house under Electronics, rank 5 glossed "to constitute"). Applied in this order by `build_vocab5k.py`:

1. **Deterministic rules** (`tools/vocab/refine.py`): gloss clean-up (wiki markup removed, verb glosses start with "to", leading "a/an/the" dropped from noun and adjective glosses, stray capitals lower-cased except proper nouns/nationalities/months); function-word typing; noun re-topic by the head noun of the first gloss clause (a list of about 1080 exact English head words such as house, dog, wine, hand, teacher, which replaces category/keyword guesses for plain nouns, never for the curated core); tags from gloss keywords.
2. **Additions** (`additions` in `data/vocab/overrides.json`, 14 words): very basic words absent from the ranked pool, found by spot-checking (for example הם "they", אנחנו "we", כל "all", מים "water", כלב "dog", מחשב "computer", רכבת "train", שולחן "table"). The pointed form is taken from Wiktionary (the gloss and topic are ours); the priority that places each one is an approximate hand-picked value, NOT a measured blended rank. Because they are inserted by priority, all ranks below roughly rank 12 shift down by up to 14 and the last 14 pool words (the previous ranks 4987-5000) fell out of the 5,000. Entry ids are hashes of (pointed form, POS) and did not change, so SRS cards keyed by id are unaffected; a word may cross a tier boundary. Round 1 ranks are not stored; the pre-refinement list is cached by the build (`tools/vocab/.cache/vocab5000.pre-refine.json`) for before/after comparison.
3. **Rank swaps** (7 pairs): the base ranking is per unpointed spelling, so a rare homograph can hold the slot earned by the common word (for example the high rank of קפה went to "money box", while "coffee" sat at 3,600; גן "gene" over "garden"; חלב "chelev" over "milk"; חם "father-in-law" over "hot"; פרק, גב, אפר). Where the common pointed word is also in the list, the pair exchanges ranks and tiers (`swapRankWith`). Other shared-spelling cases are only cross-referenced (below).
4. **Overrides** (`overrides` in `data/vocab/overrides.json`, keyed by entry id): 182 hand judgements of gloss, POS, topic, pointing, `fn`, tags or `formOf`, each with a `reason`. They win over the rules. They are one reviewer's decisions (not a Hebrew educator's), every entry stays `proofread: false`, and each carries `overridden: true`. The top 1,000 ranks were read in full (all non-verbs, and verb glosses); ranks 1,001-5,000 were checked only by a random sample of 140 entries and by the automatic checks.
5. **Forms and cross-references**:
   - `forms[]` on closed-class lemmas: Wiktionary's pronoun-suffix paradigms (for example ל: לי לך לו לה לנו לכם להם להן; של, על, אל, ב, מ, עם, את) and feminine numerals, plus זאת/אלה under זה. These are Wiktionary form-of entries restricted to non-archaic ones; they are as complete as Wiktionary, which is patchy (23 lemmas have them; many persons are missing). They are not separate learning entries.
   - `isForm: true` + `formOf: <id>` marks a list entry that is only a form of another one; the lemma gets `formIds`. Ranks are NOT changed (the form stays in its slot so the 5,000 and all tiers stay stable); consumers should skip `isForm` entries when teaching (`lemmasOnly()` in `src/vocab/vocab.js`). Only three are marked (הוה, rank 5 -> a form of היה; הוא as a rare imperative; נשים -> אשה), the cases found in the audit.
   - `sameSpelling: [ids]` lists the other entries with the same unpointed spelling (1,417 entries are in such groups). Hebrew frequency counts attach to the spelling, so these entries share one frequency and the rank of the less common ones is inflated.
6. **Function words**: closed-class entries (prepositions, conjunctions, particles, determiners, pronouns, interjections, a listed set of adverbs such as לא, גם, רק, כבר, modals and auxiliaries like היה, יכל, צריך, existential יש/אין) get `fn` (the sub-type) and `tags: ['function', ...]`; 176 entries. Their `topic` is NOT meaningful: the 27 topics are untouched and the schema requires a topic, so function words keep a topic only where they truly carry one (Time: עכשיו; Location: כאן; Directions; Pronouns; Numbers) and otherwise sit in Misc Nouns purely as a compatibility placeholder, or keep their adjective topic when they are modal adjectives. Consumers should group by the `function` tag ("Function words") and ignore `topic` for them (`isFunction()`, `contentWords()` in `vocab.js`). POS fixed for: את (particle), לא/כן/אל (particle), יש (particle, existential), כך (adverb), הן (pronoun), אף/אפילו (particle). Prefix clitics (ל ב מ ו ש ד ה כ) are tagged `clitic`; the Hebrew definite article ה is not an entry (ה in the list is the formal yes/no question prefix).
7. **Tags** (optional array, `data.tags` documents the vocabulary): `function`, `prayer`, `jewish-life` (religion words: God, priest, offering, Torah, sin, holiday...), `biblical`, `clitic`, `form`, `rare-sense`, `needs-check`, `homograph`, `colloquial`. Religion and Jewish-life words still have a topic from the 27 (mostly Society) but can now be found by tag. `prayer`, `jewish-life` and `biblical` come from gloss keywords and are heuristic (the book/prayer registry was NOT used to tag).

Rebuild: `python3 -I tools/vocab/build_vocab5k.py` (needs the cache from `fetch.py`), `node src/vocab/split-tiers.mjs`, then `node --test` and `python3 tools/vocab/quality.py`. The build is deterministic (re-running reproduces the committed file byte for byte).

## What is still unreliable

- No Hebrew educator has read any of it. The 182 overrides are also unreviewed judgement; some may be wrong. A proofreader must go through every entry; the audit only reduced the obvious errors.
- Ranks beyond 1,000 were sampled, not read. In a random sample of 140 entries from ranks 1,001-5,000, about 10% (14) had a wrong or very narrow sense, POS or topic for the pointed word shown; ten of those were fixed. This is a rough estimate from one small sample, not a measured error rate. The tail (3,000-5,000) is the weakest: rare Wiktionary first senses, loanwords, slang.
- Rank is per spelling, not per word. Shared-spelling entries (about 1,400) show the same frequency; only 7 rank swaps were made. Others are likely mis-ordered: for example `על` "yoke" at rank 407 and `רק` "saliva" at 484 ride on the frequency of the preposition and the adverb, and the pool has no common competitor to swap with. They are tagged `rare-sense`/`homograph` only where found.
- Topic is a single label with limits. About 14% of entries (759 non-function) are still in Misc Nouns, mostly abstract nouns; the head-noun list covers only the words it lists. Homograph senses and multi-sense words get the topic of the sense shown. The checks in `quality.py` use a small table independent of `refine.py`, so a zero there means "no match", not "correct".
- Function-word sub-types and the closed-adverb list are mine; interjections are included as function words. Adverbs of manner are content words.
- Lemma spellings are Wiktionary's defective (ktiv haser) forms in places (אזן, כסא, אפלו); use `pointed` for display. Three entries have no vowel points (עכשיו, יום שישי, שחור: the Wiktionary pages are unpointed and nothing was invented).
- Glosses are Wiktionary's (shortened); some are long or odd ("any instance of redness" was rewritten, others remain). 140 verb glosses are passive/stative senses ("to be calculated") which a learner may not need early; they are not filtered.
- Missing words: only 14 basics were added after spot-checking; the pool was not systematically checked for gaps (the coverage sheet shows other gaps; see COVERAGE.md), and plural nouns are generally not entries.
- Unchanged from round 1: some Wiktionary first senses are rare ones; `readList` is true when a word is in the Tanakh/prayer read list (readRank <= 3100), not a measure of how much it was boosted.
