# Voice talent buyout: term sheet for a lawyer

This is a brief of terms to request, NOT legal advice and not a contract. Have a lawyer qualified in the relevant jurisdictions (Israel for talent, plus where the publisher is based) draft and review the final agreement. Rates are UNKNOWN: the project research found no published Hebrew per-finished-hour or e-learning buyout rates.

## Rights requested
| Term | Request |
|---|---|
| Duration | Perpetual (no expiry, no renewal fee) |
| Territory | Worldwide |
| Media | All media now known or later devised, including at minimum: mobile/web app, website, print book companion media (QR/URL-linked audio), PDF and EPUB distribution including embedded audio, offline downloads, marketing and demo clips |
| Exclusivity | Non-exclusive for the talent's other work, but the talent may not license the same recordings to anyone else; consider a short category exclusivity (no competing Hebrew-learning product) for a defined period, priced separately |
| Buyout | One-time fee covering all of the above; no usage-based residuals; state explicitly that this is a total buyout |
| Derivative uses | Editing, trimming, normalization, re-encoding, splicing clips into sequences; NOT synthetic voice cloning or AI training unless separately and explicitly agreed (see below) |
| Credit | Name credit in the product credits page and book (agree wording; or anonymous at talent's option) |
| Moral rights / waiver | Include a waiver or consent to editing where the law allows; lawyer to advise on Israeli moral-rights rules |
| Re-records | Pickups and corrections of the delivered script at an agreed rate or free within a window |
| Delivery and approval | Acceptance criteria (clean audio, correct pronunciation per script); revision rounds; deadline |
| Payment | Milestones; rate card UNKNOWN; currency and tax forms TBD |
| Warranty | Talent owns/has the right to grant; no third-party content in recordings |
| Confidentiality | Mutual, limited |

## AI and synthetic voice (decide before signing)
Voice cloning or model training on the recordings is a separate, sensitive right. The 2018 dispute over Siri's Hebrew voice (a voice actor claimed her rights did not cover that use) is cited in the project research as a reason to be explicit. Default recommendation: the buyout excludes cloning and AI training; if you want that right, negotiate it as a distinct, separately priced, clearly worded grant.

## Open items for counsel
- Whether a work-for-hire or an assignment/exclusive licence is the right structure under the talent's law and the publisher's.
- Union or agency rules (e.g. agent-represented talent).
- Tax withholding for an Israeli individual.
- Separately: the redistribution rights for Azure/Google/ElevenLabs TTS output if any TTS audio ships in the app or book. No official page confirming this was found for Google Cloud or Azure; ElevenLabs says paid-plan output has commercial rights. Counsel must review the current provider terms before shipping any TTS audio.
