# Aleph donor recording guide (DRAFT, one page)

*Draft. Nobody has been contacted. Recording starts only after the donor rights grant (docs/voices/donor-rights-grant.html) is signed. Time estimates assume 120 to 180 lines per hour and are not measured.*

**Before you start.** Quiet room with soft things in it (sofa, curtains, a closet of clothes works well). Phone or fridge hum off, notifications off, windows closed. Water, no coffee or dairy right before. Sit or stand the same way for the whole session.

**Microphone.** Phone voice-recorder or computer recorder, WAV or FLAC if you can (not a voice-message app). Hold or prop the phone about **20 cm (a hand span) from your mouth**, slightly to one side to avoid puffs of air. Do a test line, listen back with headphones, then do not move it. Record **10 seconds of silence** at the start and at the end.

**Pacing.** One session is one printed sheet (about 40 lines, 15 to 25 minutes). For each line: pause one second, say it **once**, calmly and clearly, like a good teacher, not a performance; pause again. Same volume and energy for every line. Stop after 45 to 60 minutes of recording and rest.

**Reading the pointing (niqqud).** Read exactly what is written under each letter; do not read from memory.
- **Israeli:** kamatz and patach both "a"; tzere and segol both "e"; cholam "o"; kubutz and shuruk "u"; stress mostly on the last syllable.
- **Ashkenazi:** use your own community's liturgical reading, one dialect for the whole session (the director will confirm which). Typical: kamatz "o" or similar, cholam "oy" or "ey" depending on dialect, tav without a dot "s", stress on the next-to-last syllable.
- **Sephardi:** no rules decided yet; wait for the director.
- Letter-name lines: say the name once. Syllable lines: say the sound only (for example "ba"), no extra vowel on the end.

**Mistakes and retakes.** If you stumble or a noise happens, stay silent for two seconds and say the line again from the start; tick "retake" on the sheet. Never fix a line by editing. Cough or sneeze: pause, wait, repeat the line.

**After the session.** Save the file with the sheet name (for example `israeli-f-session-03.wav`), do not clean or compress it, and send it only to the person who gave you the sheet. Initial the sheet and return it.
