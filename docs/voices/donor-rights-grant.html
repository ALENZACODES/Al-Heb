Donor: the person who signs below ("you")

1. What you are doing

You will read aloud words, letters, syllables and prayer lines from a list we give you, and we will record your voice (the "Recordings"). The list is the Aleph recording script. You are not asked to say anything else.

2. What you give us

You give us a permanent, worldwide, royalty-free right to use the Recordings, including to copy, edit (cut, trim, change volume, clean up noise, join pieces together), reproduce, distribute and play them, in:

  • the Aleph Hebrew-learning app and website, whether free or paid (including in a version that is sold or subscribed to);
  • the audio that goes with the Aleph book (print, PDF, e-book, or linked audio);
  • marketing and demos for Aleph (for example short clips in adverts and social posts).

This right is non-exclusive for you: you may record for others, but you may not give these same Recordings to anyone else. We may let our service providers and successors use the Recordings for the purposes above, and we may transfer this agreement if we sell Aleph.

3. Selling the product, no royalties

You understand and agree that Aleph may be sold, including in a commercially sold product, and that the Recordings may be part of what is sold. You will not be paid royalties or a share of income. [Any one-time payment or thanks: NONE / TBD by publisher.]

4. Voice cloning and AI: separate choice (default NO)

Tick only if you agree. If you leave this blank, the answer is NO.

If the box is not ticked, we will not use the Recordings to clone your voice or to train or adjust any voice model. Your choice here does not affect the rest of this agreement or whether we accept your Recordings. [Lawyer: confirm wording and scope, including what a later model may do and for how long.]

6. Changing your mind

You may withdraw at any time by writing to [contact email]. Withdrawal stops future use: we will stop including the Recordings in new releases and new marketing as soon as reasonably practical (target: [30] days). It does not reach copies already shipped, downloaded, printed or published, and it does not undo what has already been done. [Lawyer: check this is allowed and enforceable where the donor lives.]

7. You confirm

  • You are 18 or older. (If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must also sign below and agrees to this whole agreement.)
  • The voice is yours and you are free to give these rights. You are not bound by a contract (for example an employer or an agency) that stops you.
  • You will read only what is on the script; the Recordings contain no one else's copyrighted material (the script text is supplied by us).
  • You have had the chance to ask questions and to get your own advice. Nothing was promised to you that is not written here.

8. Moral rights, privacy, law

To the extent the law allows, you consent to the editing in section 2 and agree not to assert rights that would block the uses above. We will keep your personal details (name, contact, signed form) private and use them only for this agreement and legal records.
