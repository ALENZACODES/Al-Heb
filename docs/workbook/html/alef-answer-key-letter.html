First 10 minutes: the three words

WordUnpointedSoundMeaning
שָׁלוֹםשלוםshah-LOHMpeace

מָהמהmahwhat

Check question “Which one says shalom?”: שָׁלוֹם (shah-LOHM). The other two are שָׁם there and מָה what.

The ladder pass rule is a target, not a measured result. A word read after the hint is not counted as read unpointed.
