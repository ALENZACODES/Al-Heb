Your first 10 minutes of Hebrew

Goal: Read 3 real Hebrew words aloud after 5 letters and 2 vowels, within a 10-minute budget.

Hebrew is written and read from right to left. On every practice row the black model is at the right; trace it, then keep writing to the left as the letters fade from grey, to dotted, to empty.

The plan (10 minutes)

1 minHebrew reads right to left. Start at the right edge. You will learn 5 letters and 2 vowel signs, then read 3 real words.
2 minFive letters. shin, lamed, vav, mem, he
1.5 minTwo vowel signs. kamatz, holam-male
1.5 minRead your first word. word: shalom
1 minRead a second word. word: sham
1 minRead a third word. word: mah
1.5 minWhich one says shalom?. shalom / sham / mah
0.5 minYou read 3 Hebrew words. Self-reported. Nothing here has been tested with learners yet.

DRAFT, unreviewed by any Hebrew teacher. Pronunciation shown is Israeli only. Not user-tested; the 10-minute figure is a design budget, not a measured result. Only one of the three words (shalom) is in the Priestly Blessing. The blessing's other words need letters (yod, bet, resh, kaf, final kaf, nun, and so on) and vowels (tzere, segol, shva) outside the 5 letters and 2 vowels taught here. Extending to the full verse is a later step.

A vowel sign sits under (or, for the vav-with-dot, next to) the letter. Trace the pairs, then the words. Models use the print font so the marks are clear.

ah · kamatzKamatz: the T-shaped sign under the letter.
oh · holam-maleVav with a dot above it reads as the vowel oh; the vav is not a v here.

Read and write three words

שָׁלוֹם
1. shah-LOHM = peace
Letters, right to left: Shin, Lamed, Vav, Mem. Priestly Blessing, Numbers 6:26 (last word); also Oseh Shalom.
Without the vowel signs it is written שלום.
שָׁם
2. shahm = there
Letters, right to left: Shin, Mem. Common word, not from the Priestly Blessing.
Without the vowel signs it is written שם.
מָה
3. mah = what
Letters, right to left: Mem, He. Common word, not from the Priestly Blessing.
Without the vowel signs it is written מה.
