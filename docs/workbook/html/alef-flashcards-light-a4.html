Vocabulary flashcards

1,000 common Hebrew words as cut-out cards, 12 per page, most frequent first inside each theme. 934 words are grouped by theme (data/vocab/themes); the rest are in the last group.

How to use

  1. Print single-sided at 100% (Actual size) on card or plain paper and cut along the grey lines.
  2. Read the Hebrew aloud, then turn to the meaning: the transliteration and English are on the card to check yourself, or fold or cover them.
  3. Sort into "know" and "learning" piles. The small number is the word's frequency rank.
