Notes where the traditions differ.

8 Chet. S pharyngeal [ħ], a throat sound (the ch of Bach / loch is the Israeli merged value).

16 Ayin. S pharyngeal [ʕ] (Western Sephardim [ŋ]); silent in Israeli and Ashkenazi.

20 Resh. I Real sound: back-of-the-throat r (like French or German r), not the English r · S Real sound: front r, rolled or tapped (like Spanish r) · A Real sound: r varies by regional dialect: front (alveolar) or back-of-the-throat (uvular)

Three ways to say it. I Israeli: everyday modern Hebrew (the default here). S Sephardi, as the app's setting: Israeli-style vowels said with English-speaking habits. A Ashkenazi: the traditional siddur reading, for example Shabbos for Shabbat. Pick one and keep to it. The picture hooks use the Israeli sound.
