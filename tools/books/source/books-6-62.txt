# Format: "## id | titleHe | titleTranslit | titleEn | band" then one page per line: "he | translit | en | illustration".
# Bands: A = 2 to 3 words a page, B = 3 to 5, C = 4 to 6 (sometimes two short sentences).
# All stories are original. Drafts: no native speaker has reviewed the Hebrew or the pointing.

Moses worried that he did not know how to speak well, but he received a promise: Aaron, his brother, would help him. | Moses kneeling barefoot before the burning bush

Ha-mayim ba-y'or hafchu la-adumim, tz'farde'im kaftzu l'chol makom, afilu l'toch ha-mitah shel Par'oh, v'choshech gadol kisah et kol Mitzrayim. Makah acharei makah, v'lev Par'oh nishar kasheh. Rak acharei eser makot amar sof sof: "L'chu!" B'nei Yisrael yatz'u b'chipazon kol kach gadol, she-ha-batzek shelahem lo hispik litpo'ach. V'chach noldah ha-matzah. | Then the plagues began. The water in the Nile turned red, frogs hopped everywhere, even into Pharaoh's bed, and a great darkness covered all of Egypt. Plague after plague, Pharaoh's heart stayed hard. Only after ten plagues did he finally say, "Go!" The Children of Israel left in such a great hurry that their dough had no time to rise. And that is how matzah was born. | Frogs in Pharaoh's bedroom, and families leaving with unleavened dough
בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל הִגִּיעוּ עַד יַם סוּף, וּפִתְאוֹם שָׁמְעוּ מֵאֲחוֹרֵיהֶם אֶת הַמֶּרְכָּבוֹת שֶׁל פַּרְעֹה. הָאֲנָשִׁים פָּחֲדוּ, אֲבָל מֹשֶׁה אָמַר: "אַל תִּירָאוּ!" הוּא נָטָה אֶת יָדוֹ עַל הַיָּם, וֶאֱלֹהִים שָׁלַח רוּחַ קָדִים חֲזָקָה כָּל הַלַּיְלָה. הַמַּיִם נִבְקְעוּ וְעָמְדוּ כְּמוֹ חוֹמָה מִיָּמִין וּמִשְּׂמֹאל, וּבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל עָבְרוּ בְּשָׁלוֹם בַּיַּבָּשָׁה בְּתוֹךְ הַיָּם. פַּרְעֹה וְכָל חֵילוֹ רָדְפוּ אַחֲרֵיהֶם אֶל תּוֹךְ הַיָּם. אָז אָמַר אֱלֹהִים לְמֹשֶׁה: "נְטֵה אֶת יָדְךָ עַל הַיָּם, וְיָשֻׁבוּ הַמַּיִם עַל מִצְרַיִם." מֹשֶׁה נָטָה אֶת יָדוֹ, וְלִפְנוֹת בֹּקֶר חָזַר הַיָּם לִמְקוֹמוֹ. הַגַּלִּים כִּסּוּ אֶת הַמֶּרְכָּבוֹת וְאֶת הַפָּרָשִׁים, וּפַרְעֹה וְכָל חֵילוֹ טָבְעוּ בַּיָּם. לֹא נִשְׁאַר בָּהֶם עַד אֶחָד. | B'nei Yisrael higi'u ad Yam Suf, u-fit'om sham'u me-achoreihem et ha-merkavot shel Par'oh. Ha-mayim nivk'u v'amdu k'mo chomah mi-yamin u-mi-s'mol, u-v'nei Yisrael avru b'shalom ba-yabashah b'toch ha-yam. Par'oh v'chol cheilo radfu achareihem el toch ha-yam. Az amar Elohim l'Moshe: "N'teh et yad'cha al ha-yam, v'yashuvu ha-mayim al Mitzrayim." Moshe natah et yado, v'lifnot boker chazar ha-yam lim'komo. Ha-galim kisu et ha-merkavot v'et ha-parashim, u-Far'oh v'chol cheilo tav'u ba-yam. Lo nishar bahem ad echad. | The Children of Israel reached the Sea of Reeds, and suddenly they heard Pharaoh's chariots behind them. The people were afraid, but Moses said, "Do not be afraid!" He stretched out his hand over the sea, and God sent a strong east wind all night long. The waters split apart and stood like a wall on the right and on the left, and the Children of Israel crossed safely on dry land through the middle of the sea. Pharaoh and his whole army chased after them into the sea. Then God said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, and let the waters come back over Egypt."
